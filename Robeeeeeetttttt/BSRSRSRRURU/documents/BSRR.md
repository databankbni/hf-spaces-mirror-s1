**SHIP RECYCLING RULES, 2025 (DRAFT)**

In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 54 (1) of the Bangladesh Ship Recycling (Amendment) Act, 2025 the Government of Bangladesh hereby enacts these Rules to regulate all ship recycling activities within Bangladesh and to give full and complete effect of the Hong Kong International Convention for the Safe and Environmentally Sound Recycling of Ships, 2009, along with its associated guidelines.

**CHAPTER I**

**Initial**

**1. Short title and introduction** . –

(1) These Rules may be called the Bangladesh Ship Recycling Rules, 2025.

(2) It shall come into force on a date designated by the Government through notification in the Official Gazette.

**2. Application:** (1) Unless otherwise expressly provided, the provisions of this Rule shall apply to— Any ship not less than 500 GT including warship, naval auxiliary or other ship owned or operated by an Administration including Government non-commercial service, and which is destined for recycling in a ship recycling facility operating within the jurisdiction of Bangladesh.

(2) Notwithstanding anything contained herein, the Ministry of Shipping, Government of Bangladesh, and the respective Port Authorities or relevant Government Department may, with the consent of the Ministry of Industries, grant specific exemptions for recycling or breaking of ships grounded outside a recognized ship recycling plot or SRF. These Rules shall not apply to ships classified as wrecks, for which the provisions of the Merchant Shipping Ordinance, 1983, shall govern removal

(3) A ship delivered physically to the facility for recycling shall not be subject to any claim or maritime lien arising against any former owner of ship

**3. Definition** . – The words and expressions defined in section 2 of the Act shall have the same meaning as in this Rule and, unless there is anything repugnant in the subject or context, in this Rule

(1) “Act" means the “Bangladesh Ship Recycling (Amendment) Act 2025"

(2) “Rule" means the “Bangladesh Ship Recycling Rules, 2025” for safe and environmentally sound ship recycling in Bangladesh

(3) “Ship" as used in this Rule means vessels and floating structures of any type operated in the marine environment and shall include submersibles, floating crafts, floating platforms, self-elevating platforms, floating Storage Unit, floating production storage units and offloading units, a vessel stripped of equipment or being towed; destined for breaking purpose.

(4) "Tanker" means a vessel designed for transporting liquid bulk cargo, including oil, chemicals, and liquefied gases.

(5) “Ship Recycling Facility” means a defined area that is a site, SRF or facility approved by the Ship Recycling Board and used for the recycling of ships

(6) “Convention” means The Hong Kong International Convention for the Safe and Environmentally Sound Recycling of Ships adopted in 2009.

(7) “Basel Convention” means The Basel Convention on the Control of Transboundary Movements of Hazardous Wastes and their Disposal adopted in 1989

(8) "MRCC" means the Maritime Rescue Coordination Centre (MRCC), which is an institution / organization engaged on a regular basis for the protection of life and property at sea, such as the Navy, the Coast Guard or the Port Authority.

(9) "SRFP" means the "Ship Recycling Facility Plan" as defined in section 2 (8) of the Act or the plan with stipulated conditions, promulgated by the Board following the guidelines adopted by the relevant MEPC resolution as applicable, on the basis of which a ship recycling facility is authorised to act.

(10)"SRP" means a "ship recycling plan" as defined in section 2 (7) of the Act or a plan prepared in accordance with the prescribed guidelines or notification as promulgated by the Board following the guidelines adopted by the relevant MEPC resolution as applicable.

(11)"Association" means the association / association of Ship Recycling Facility owners who are engaged in ship recycling and duly approved by the SRB;

(12) "Inspector" means a person appointed or authorized by the Government or the Competent Authority under the Bangladesh Ship Recycling Act, 2018, to inspect, monitor, and enforce compliance with ship recycling regulations, including environmental, safety, and health standards, in accordance with national laws and the Hong Kong Convention, 2009 *.*

(13) "Safety Officer" means any person employed by the SRF owner in-charge of Safety, Health and Environment (SHE) prevention and mitigation measures from the panel of SBB

(14) "Competent Authority" shall refer to the Ship Recycling Board (SRB), a governmental authority represented by its Director General, empowered to regulate, supervise, and monitor ship recycling operations within the jurisdiction of Bangladesh in accordance with the provisions of the Hong Kong Convention and the guidelines adopted by the relevant MEPC resolution as applicable.

(15) 'LDT' means the Light Displacement Tonnage which is the weight of the ship and of the attached / outfitting goods, including all connected, fitting fixings of the ship. That is, the weight determined by Archimedes' formula. However, fuel oil and lubes oil, ballast water, feed water and consumables shall be excluded from the LDT of the vessel;

(16) "TSDF" means a treatment, storage and disposal facility where hazardous wastes are treated, stored, destroyed and reused in an environmentally sound manner.

(17) “Competent person”: A person with suitable training, and sufficient knowledge, experience and skill, for the performance of the specific work involved in a ship recycling facility, in good safety conditions. The Ship Recycling Board shall define appropriate criteria for the designation of such persons and shall determine the duties to be assigned to them and

(18) “Administration” means the Government of the State whose flag the ship is entitled to fly, or under whose authority it is operating.

(19) "Port Authority" means a Port officer or any other person appointed and authorized by the Ministry of Shipping.

(20) “Organization” means the International Maritime Organization (IMO)

(21) "inspection" means the inspection of imported or collected vessels for recycling purposes in order to check whether the recycling and related activities are being carried out properly in accordance with the Rules;

(22) "International Certificate on Inventory of Hazardous materials (IHM)" means a certificate, which encompasses the hazardous materials on board the ship in the format as prescribed by the SRB in accordance with import policy and the Hong Kong Convention along with the guidelines adopted by the relevant MEPC resolution as applicable.

(23) "PPE" means Personal Protective Equipment which is used for protection against accident, fire, gas, chemical infection or any other injury to persons engaged in SRF operation, inspection, partitioning activities, loading and unloading of goods, etc. According to the type of work, helmets, boots, belts, masks, gowns, goggles, hand gloves, life jackets, buoys, gas masks, ladders, gangways, automatic baskets for boarding the ship, etc. will also be included.

(24) "Bangladesh Maritime Zone" means the Bangladesh Maritime Zone as defined in Sec 2(12) of the Territorial Waters and Maritime Zones (Amendment) Act, 2021."

(25) "Carried goods" means goods transported by ship." which is transported by ship from one country or port to another;

(26) "Ship Recycling" is the process of dismantling a vessel's structure or disposal whether conducted at a beach or green recycling facility for dismantling ship. It includes a wide range of activities, from removing all gears and equipment to cut down and recycle the ship's infrastructure in a safe and an environmentally sound manner.

(27) "Ship recycling activities" means and include all activities such as beaching, cutting, dismantling of the ship and disposal of all dismantled materials from the SRF in safe and environmentally sound manner.

(28) "Beaching" in the context of shipbreaking refers to the process of deliberately grounding a vessel onto a designated shore or tidal mudflat to facilitate its dismantling and recycling in a ship recycling facility.

(29) "Green Ship Recycling Methods" refers to environmentally sustainable ship dismantling techniques, including dry-docking, pier breaking, top-down, and slipway methods, ensuring safety, pollution control, and hazardous waste management

(30) “Green Recycling Facility” means a ship recycling facility with designated infrastructure ensuring equipped with designated infrastructure such as SRF s, docks, dockside areas, slips, or floating working platforms for full containment of liquids, particulate matter, and hazardous residues, with systems to prevent, capture, and manage any accidental emissions or discharges.

(31) "Vendor" means the persons and institutions engaged in the purchase, sale and management of various products produced from the ship recycling process or the persons and institutions engaged in the management of waste produced from ship recycling and the contractors approved by the Labour Directorate supplying workers to the SRF;"

(32) "duty tax" "means the duty tax determined on the basis of the weight of the vessel / LDT of the vessel, etc.;"

(33) "worker" "means a worker as defined in the Labour Act, 2006 (Act No. 42 of 2006);"

(34) "Convention" means the “Hong Kong International Convention for the Safe &amp; Environmentally Sound Recycling of Ships 2009"

(35) “DASR” means document of authorization to conduct ship recycling granted as a certificate of authorization in accordance with Rule 7.

(36) “Provisional DASR” means a temporary authorization granted by the Competent Authority— (a) to an existing Ship Recycling Facility (SRF) that applies within six months of the enforcement of the BSSR 2025 to conduct ship recycling pending full compliance; and (b) to a new SRF to undertake all activities related to ship recycling except for ship cutting and hazardous waste removal, unless such restrictions are explicitly waived by the Competent Authority based on demonstrated advance compliance with relevant provisions of these Rules.

(37) “Site inspection” means an inspection of the Ship Recycling Facility confirming the condition described by the verified documentation

(38) “Statement of Completion” means a confirmatory statement issued by the Ship Recycling Facility that the Ship Recycling has been completed in accordance with this Convention.

(39) “Recycling Company” means the owner of the Ship Recycling Facility or any other organization or person who has assumed the responsibility for operation of the Ship Recycling activity from the owner of the Ship Recycling Facility and who on assuming such responsibility has agreed to take over all duties and responsibilities imposed by this Convention.

(40) "Authorized Ship Recycling Facility" means a ship recycling facility or SRF under the ship recycling zone to cater safe and environmentally sound ship recycling activities approved by SBB.

(41) “Safe for man entry” means that in the compartment or space so designated; the oxygen content of the atmosphere is at least 19.5 percent and not more than 23 percent by volume, toxic materials in the atmosphere are within permissible concentrations and the residues cannot produce toxic materials under existing atmospheric conditions

(42) "Safe for hot work" refers to a designation for a compartment or space wherein the atmospheric oxygen content is maintained at not less than 19.5 percent and not more than 23 percent by volume, except in the case of inert spaces or where external hot work is to be conducted. The concentration of flammable substances in the atmosphere shall be below 10 percent of the lower flammable limit, and existing residues shall not generate a higher concentration under prevailing atmospheric conditions in the presence of fire. Additionally, all adjacent spaces that contain or have previously contained flammable or combustible substances must be adequately cleaned to preclude the propagation of fire, or must be rendered inert to a satisfactory extent. In the case of fuel tanks, lube oil tanks, engine room bilges, or boiler room bilges, such spaces shall be treated appropriately to facilitate the safe execution of hot work.

(43) “Vital record” means all important and written documents and records associated with Facility operations and, specifically, the recycling of each ship which may include, but not be limited to, different safety procedures observed at the SRF including hot-works, enclosed space entry, laboratory analytical results, manifests, shipping documents, truck receipts, waste shipment records, records of training and exercises/drills, worker accidents, injuries and medical or health records such as occupational health examinations carried out and occupational diseases contracted, and a description of any national requirements for records management and retention.

(44) “Prescribed” means prescribed by decisions, notifications or circulations of the Ship Recycling Board made under this Rule;

(45) “Non-party ship” refers to a ship that flies the flag of a nation that has neither ratified nor acceded to the Hong Kong Convention.

(46) “NOC” means No Objection Certificate. It is mandatory to obtain from SBB to import the particular ship for scraping in Bangladesh

(47) “Fee” prescribes for obtaining required permission under these Rules.

(48) “Board” means the Ship Recycling Board, which operates under the supervisory authority of the Ministry of Industries for regulating and overseeing matters related to ship recycling, in accordance with the provisions of the Ship Recycling Act and these Rules.

(49) "Determination" means the process by which the Competent Authority(ies) decides whether to issue, amend, suspend, withdraw or renew a DASR

(50) “Audit” means the process of systematically examining, inspecting, and evaluating the activities or operational procedures of any individual, organization, or entity to assess their accuracy, transparency, and compliance. Such audit is ordinarily conducted by an independent and professional auditor to determine whether the applicable laws, rules, or standards have been duly followed, and whether the information or data presented by the entity is accurate, reliable, and credible.

(51) MEPC refers to the Marine Environment Protection Committee of the International Maritime Organization (IMO) **,** responsible for developing and overseeing environmental regulations, including those related to ship recycling under the HKC.

(52) “Ship’s Inbuilt Structure” refers to the fundamental components that constitute the vessel’s physical integrity and structural framework. This includes, but is not limited to, the ship’s hull, bulkheads, decks, structural beams, integral tanks, and permanently affixed piping or machinery essential to the ship’s design and function as a maritime vessel. Any material, equipment, or substance that is not an intrinsic part of this structural framework shall not be considered part of the ship’s inbuilt structure.

(53) “Ships tank” refers to all enclosed compartments designed for storing liquids, gases, or other substances during a vessel’s operation. These include fuel oil tanks for propulsion fuel, bunker tanks for stored fuel supply, ballast tanks for stability management, cargo tanks for liquid cargo such as oil or chemicals, slop tanks for oil-contaminated residues, waste oil and oily water tanks for bilge and engine waste, and sewage or grey water tanks for wastewater storage.

(54) The words and expressions used and not defined in this Rule but defined in the Petroleum Act, 1934, Explosive Act, 1884, Bangladesh Environment Conservation Act, 1995 (Act, 1 of 1995 amended on 2010), Labor Act, 2006, Labour Rule 2015, Bangladesh Merchant Shipping Ordnance 1983 , The Inland Water Transport Authority Ordinance, 1958, Import Policy Order, ILO Guidelines for safety and health in ship breaking activities, The Hong Kong International Convention for safe and environmentally sound recycling of ships, 2009, Marine Environment Protection Committee Resolutions as applicable, Basel Convention 1989 shall have the same meanings respectively assigned to them in those Acts

(55) “Recognized Organization” means an independent entity that sets standards and conducts verification related to the design, construction, and maintenance of ships, marine structures, and industrial engineering systems. Such organizations ensure safety and operational integrity in accordance with international standards and assess the technical adequacy of relevant equipment and structures.

**CHAPTER II**

**Leasing of zoned land &amp; setting up of SRF**

**4. Leasing of zoned land for setting up of SRF.** – (1) The Director General on behalf of the Board shall divide the zoned land declared under Section-4 of the Act and Rule-4 of the Rules in the form of plots for the purpose of establishing SRF s and shall mark each eligible plot with a number. The details of each plot of land, such as the name of the mouza, the Daag number, the ledger number, the area of the land, the ownership of the land, the nature of the land, etc., shall be recorded in a register. The plot number given by the Board shall not be inconsistent with the plot number given by the Land Survey Department in the map.

(2) The geographical nature of the zoned land, environmental and environmental sensitivity, seashore condition and depth, transportation system, land ownership, etc. will be considered when dividing it into plots. The plot size can be reduced or increased as needed. When plots are divided, SRF s approved and established before adoption of these regulations shall be maintained as far as possible.

(3) In the event of land acquisition for the declaration of a new zone or the leasing of existing government-owned or public land, the relevant provisions of the Bangladesh Environment Conservation Act, 2010, and the Environment Conservation Rules, 2023, shall be strictly adhered to and duly complied with.

(4). On behalf of the Board, the Director General shall, with the prior approval of the Government, lease the zoned plots divided in accordance with sub-rules (1) and (2) to the applicant as person / institution for the purpose of setting up the SRF subject to the ownership of the Ministry of Industries. In case of grant of lease, preference shall be given to the following persons / entities subject to fulfilment of other conditions relating to grant of lease:

(i) the person / organization operating the SRF on the plot concerned since before the coming into force of these Rules.

(ii) the owner of the land concerned or the person or organization contracting with the owner of the land, if the plot is owned by a person.

(5) If the plot is wholly owned by the Ministry of Industries, the Board shall issue a public notice for the lease of such plot. The conditions of the notification and the eligibility and priority of the lease will be determined by the Board with the approval of the Government.

(6) If any person with partial ownership or institutions referred to in sub-rules (4) (i) and (4) (ii) do not accept the lease within 5 (five) years of the coming into force of these Rules, the Director General on behalf of the Board shall, after giving notice to the land owners concerned and after obtaining their consent, grant the lease in accordance with the open lease procedure under sub-rule (5). In such cases, the land owners concerned shall be entitled to a proportionate share of the lease price excluding the development cost and the cost applicable to the leasing process.

(7) The lease shall not be transferable in any manner.

(8) The lease period of the plot shall ordinarily be 20 (twenty) years and the lease period shall be extended by 5 (five) years each time the SRF is operated on a regular basis and in good standing as decided by the Board.

(9) The Government shall fix the annual lease price on the basis of the area of the plotted land and the length of the sea shore. Provided that in terms of sub-clauses 4 (i) and (ii), the lessee shall be exempted from payment of lease price for the land owned by the person concerned. The lease value of 3 (three) years shall be paid together at the time of first lease and thereafter the lease value shall be paid every year (before 30th June).

(10) The lease of the leased plot shall be terminated for any of the following reasons unless, in the Board's opinion, valid grounds exist for not doing so:

(i) failure to pay the rent for two consecutive years;

(ii) failure to commence the operation of the SRF within 3 (three) years of taking over the lease, except in the case of sub-rule 4 (i);

(iii) the abandonment of the ship recycling business by the lessee / institution;

(iv) if the business is not conducted on the leased plot for two consecutive years without any valid justification;

(v) the order of permanent closure of the SRF issued by the Government under sub-rule 35 of Rule 7.

(vi) Transfer of leased land.

(vii) Sublet / rent the leased land.

**CHAPTER III**

**General provisions**

**5. Obligations of the Ship Recycling Board— The Ship Recycling Board, as the Competent Authority, shall perform the following duties, namely: —**

(1) Oversee SRF activities related to workers' safety, ensure the implementation of adequate measures and facilities for the handling, management, and disposal of hazardous wastes or materials in shipbreaking SRF s in compliance with the provisions of the Act and the Convention, and nothing shall preclude the Board from adopting standards exceeding those prescribed by the Convention.

(2) function as a One-Stop Service provider under the Ministry of Industries *,* delivering integrated services for ship breaking, recycling, and related operations.

(3) authorize a ship recycling facility, approve ship recycling plan and issue permission for ship recycling in the manner specified in regulations;

(4) ensure that the person or persons engaged for the purpose of subrule (3) are duly qualified;

(5) Submit a copy of the Statement of Completion, as prescribed by the Board, to the Ministry of Industries and the Administration within fourteen days of its receipt from the Ship Recycler, using the format following the Convention and as specified in Appendix 7 of ANNEX of the Convention

(6) ensure workers involved in recycling activities undergo regular medical examination and approved training for the delegated tasks, duties and responsibilities in the manner as specified by the Board;

(7) carry out enquiry or inspection in the manner as prescribed by the guidelines adopted by the relevant MEPC resolution as applicable and the Rules;

(8) ensure that the worker’s compensations as prescribed in this Rule are duly and promptly paid;

(9) maintain all vital records, audit reports, copy of the DASR and such other authorizations granted under the Act for a period of at least of five years from the date of an audit or from date of the grant of the DASR or such other authorizations, as the case may be;

(10) Ensure that organizations appointed on behalf of the Board for the approval of the Ship Recycling Plan (SRP) or Ship Recycling Facility Plan (SRFP), if any, are consistent with and not in conflict with the organizations recognized by the Flag State Administration or the Maritime Administration of Bangladesh;

(11) carry out environmental impact assessment at intervals not exceeding two years in the manner as prescribed by the Board;

(12) requisition services of agencies prescribed under section 48 of the Act ;

(13) carry out any other duties assigned by authority the Ministry of Industries which are consistent with the provisions of the Act and the Convention and relevant guidelines on ship recycling as adopted by different international organizations.

(14) The Board shall designate a Single Contact Point for communication with the Organization, Parties to this Convention, and other relevant entities concerning matters related to Ship Recycling Facilities operating within the jurisdiction of Bangladesh. The designated Single Contact Point shall ensure compliance with all reporting requirements and other obligations as stipulated in the Hong Kong Convention and the guidelines adopted by the relevant MEPC resolution as applicable

(15) The Board, with the Ministry of Industries’ approval, may notify /announce additional requirements not covered in the Act, Rules, or guidelines for the safe and environmentally sound recycling of ships in Bangladesh, provided that such announcements align with the objectives of the Act and the Convention.

(16) The Board shall determine the terms of reference for the Safety Agency, including the qualifications, skills, and experience required for its Safety officers /inspectors, and may revise such criteria as deemed necessary. The Board shall be responsible for supervising the Safety Agency. If any Safety Agency is found negligent in performing its duties as prescribed or violates any conditions imposed by the Board, the Director General may impose a fine, revoke its appointment, and declare it ineligible for future designation as a Safety Agency.

(17) The fee of the Safety Agency shall be determined by the Ministry of Industries. The fee shall be directly submitted to the board by the facility owners. The Board shall pay their dues in accordance with the terms of the agreement entered into with the Safety Agency.

**(18) The Board shall establish technical competency guidelines and constitute a panel of Safety Agencies comprising qualified Safety Officers, based on an assessment of their qualifications. The Board shall assign Safety Officers to oversee the dismantling of ships authorized by the Board. Such Safety Officers shall function as the technical arm of the Board and shall be subject to a reporting obligation to the Board. Furthermore, Safety Officers shall have the authority to temporarily suspend cutting operations if the corrective measures prescribed by the relevant authority are not implemented within the stipulated timeframe, as determined by the Board.**

(i) The engagement of Safety Officers shall be mandatory for ship recyclers, who shall receive technical guidance from them on ship recycling operations. Safety Officers shall assist in SRFP and SRP preparation, ship cutting management, waste handling, and occupational health and safety. They may also support emergency management when required. The Board shall disburse their remuneration, which shall be recoverable from the respective SRF (s).

(ii) Safety Officers shall function as independent contractors with an advisory role. Ship recyclers may not attribute liability to the Board for any wrongful act or omission by a Safety Officer *.*

(iii) The Board shall appoint and renew their contracts annually, subject to performance evaluation and determination of fitness and propriety at the Board’s discretion.

(19) The Board shall determine a class of work by determining the minimum qualification according to the type of ship recycling work and provide suitable training to the workers to acquire the necessary skills according to the class of work and.

(20) In addition to the training institute established under Section 19(2) of the Act, any training institute established through private initiatives or public-private partnerships may, subject to the approval of the Board, provide training to workers and employees engaged in the ship recycling industry.

(21) The Board, with the approval of the Ministry of Industries, shall develop and prescribe the necessary training curriculum for ship recycling workers, ensuring classification into distinct categories based on their functions, required skill sets, occupational hazards, and working-class format. The Board shall review and update the training curriculum periodically to align with evolving industry standards, technological advancements, and regulatory requirements.

(22) The training curriculum shall be formulated in consultation with relevant authorities, industry experts, and occupational health and safety professionals to ensure that it adequately addresses technical competencies, occupational safety protocols, and environmental compliance requirements.

(23) The training institutes established under Section 19(2) of the Act, as well as any approved private or joint-initiative training institutes, shall conduct training programs strictly in accordance with the curriculum prescribed by the Board.

(24) The training curricula shall be designed to ensure that all workers acquire the requisite technical skills, occupational safety knowledge, and environmental awareness, in compliance with the Hong Kong International Convention for the Safe and Environmentally Sound Recycling of Ships, 2009, and all other applicable national and international standards.

(25) The Board shall preserve and publish the information of the workers and employees who have passed the training from the training institutions mentioned in section 19 of the Act in a suitable database and update it regularly. However, the Board may, if it deems necessary, determine the criteria for inclusion in the database of workers and employees.

(26) The Board shall maintain a registry of workers, documenting the work experience of all employees, whether casual or regular, in any shipbreaking facility that is a member of the registered trade body of ship recyclers and compliant with HKC standards, certified by a recognized organization.

(27) The Board, in coordination with the Civil Surgeon and the Health Department of the Government, shall establish a database containing the health profiles and health cards of all workers engaged in the ship recycling industry to determine their baseline health status.

(28) The Board shall determine the minimum qualifications for appointment to the organogram and technical posts of the technical manpower to be appointed in the SRF. The opinion of the concerned stakeholders may be taken in determining such organogram and eligibility for appointment.

(29) The Board shall maintain a panel of Beaching Masters among the valid class one Master’s certificate of competency. Qualification of beaching master will be determined by Board in consultation with relevant stakeholders. Beaching fee will be decided by Board according to the size and type of the Ship.

(30) To mitigate operational risks in the handling of used oil/sludge, hazardous waste and materials (particularly asbestos), and cable management at operational ship recycling facilities, the Board shall maintain a list of vendors proficient in these tasks. Ship recyclers shall exclusively engage these vendors for such hazardous operations. Vendors shall be selected and registered by the Board in accordance with its prescribed directives.

(31) The Board shall implement adequate measures for ships below 500 GT and not classified under this Rule, ensuring their disposal does not provide unnecessary health risks to the workers involved and/or the environment concerned.

(32) The Board shall undertake periodic and comprehensive assessments to enhance ship recycling methodologies and practices. It shall be vested with a continuous mandate to promote and implement environmentally sustainable and green ship recycling methods, with the objective of achieving "zero waste" and "zero accidents" on an annual basis.

(33) The Board shall carry out any other duties assigned by the Ministry of Industries which are consistent with the provisions of the Act and the Convention.

(34) The Board shall treat all ships destined for recycling in Bangladesh equally in terms of compliance with the Act and all Rules and orders made under this Act, irrespective of their party or non-party status.

(35) The Board, representing the Government, is empowered to investigate and address violations or attempted violations of Convention obligations by ship recyclers in Bangladesh upon receiving substantiated complaints from any party state. This includes providing a report to the complainant and the organization detailing actions taken within one year of receiving the complaint.

(36) The Board, representing the Government, should disseminate information on the list of ship recycling facilities it has approved, any violations of convention obligations by these facilities, and any actions taken against them to address the Organization.

(37) The Board, representing the Government, shall make every effort to resolve any dispute with the other party concerning the Convention by peaceful resolution.

(38) For obtaining permits, certifications, and other services from the Ship Recycling Board (SRB), fees shall be payable as determined by the Government through official gazette notification. Fees may be levied for the following:

(a) Ship Recycling Facility Plan (SRFP): Fees shall be applicable for validation for a period of five (5) years and for biannual (six-monthly) inspections. Such inspections shall include, but not be limited to, baseline surveys, compliance surveys, periodic and special surveys, accident investigations, corrective measure assessments, checklist compliance verifications, documentation reviews, and certification processes.

(b) Ship Survey and Inspection: Fees shall be payable for ship surveys and inspections, including arrangements for vessel visits, examinations, testing, evaluation, and assessment for beaching permission.

(c) Beaching and Cutting Permission: Fees shall be applicable for facilitating beaching permissions, appointing Beaching Masters and Safety Officers, engaging agencies for used oil/sludge handling, hazardous waste abatement (including asbestos management), cable handling, and vendor registration. Additionally, fees shall be charged for the use of the Treatment, Storage, and Disposal Facility (TSDF) and Central Incinerator, ensuring safety compliance during cutting operations, documentation processing, checklist orientations, and certification issuance.

(39) The Board shall collect and analyse data on accidents and incidents occurring in Bangladesh and other ship recycling countries. Based on the nature of such occurrences, the Board shall share relevant information, ensuring the exclusion of any identifying data, and issue precautionary notices to ship recyclers as necessary to prevent similar accidents.

(40) The Board shall establish systems for the evaluation, control, and audit of organizations recognized by the competent authority to ensure their effective supervision.

(41) The Board shall establish, oversee the Common Treatment Storage and Disposal Facility (TSDF), for the use of all industries including the ship recycling industry and necessary regulations and guidelines in this regard should be promulgated for this purpose through appropriate gazette notifications.

(42) Recognizing the interdisciplinary nature of the Common Treatment, Storage, and Disposal Facility (TSDF) and the necessity for coordinated governance, the oversight and regulatory supervision of TSDF operations shall be entrusted to an inter-ministerial committee constituted for this purpose.

(43) The inter-ministerial committee, constituted under the Board, shall consist of representatives deputed from the Ministry of Industries, the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, the Ministry of Shipping, and other relevant ministries and agencies, to ensure effective oversight of the environmental and industrial aspects of TSDF operations.

(44) The committee constituted under sub-rule (42) shall be responsible for coordinating with the Department of Environment in the formulation of policies, establishment of technical and standard operational guidelines, compliance monitoring, and granting approvals for any deviations or special considerations related to the management and governance of TSDF, in accordance with the applicable legal framework.

(45) The decisions and directives of the committee regarding TSDF governance &amp; operation shall be binding on all ship recycling facilities and relevant stakeholders, and each SRF must become a member of Treatment Storage and Disposal Facility (TSDF) authorized by Board. Ship recyclers shall have to pay for waste management charges to the common TSDF operators, based on the actual quantities of wastes disposed as instructed by the Board.

(46) All sub-rules contained within these Rules shall be read in conjunction with, and in conformity to, the provisions of Section 11 of the Act.

**6. Obligations of Ship Recyclers:** (1) A Ship Recycler shall ensure that all temporary and permanent workers employed in a ship recycling facility are insured against disability and death, and are reimbursed in accordance with the Bangladesh Labour Act 2006 and the Insurance Act 2010. The owner of the SRF shall remit the insurance premium for the applicable period or year of the employee. The amount, extent and manner in which such compensation shall be insured by the Ship Recycler shall depend on the nature of the delegated tasks, duties and responsibilities as prescribed by the Board.

(2) The SRF shall ensure that the seller, cash buyer, or agent implements local oil spill contingency plans to effectively prevent, control, and mitigate any oil spill occurring from the anchoring point to the SRF. Such plans shall comply with applicable national and international environmental regulations and shall be subject to monitoring and enforcement by the relevant authorities.

(3) A Ship Recycler shall maintain individual or comprehensive insurance coverage in an amount and manner determined by the Board to compensate for any harm to the population in the vicinity, environmental damage, and clean-up operation costs arising from violations of any provisions enshrined in chapter 6 of this Rule.

(4)The owner of the ship recycling yard shall maintain valid insurance coverage in accordance with the following international conventions, or other applicable conventions:

(i) The International Convention on Civil Liability for Oil Pollution Damage, 1992;
    (ii) The International Convention on Civil Liability for Bunker Oil Pollution Damage, 2001.

Explanation: For the purposes of this sub-rule, "environmental damage" shall include—
    (iii) any damage to the environment or property arising from the discharge of substances from the ship recycling facility or the recyclable ship;

If any SRF violates this, the Board can take punitive action as described in the Bangladesh Ship Recycling Act 2018 and this Rule, including not granting permission to import ships in its favour.

(6) wages of the workers. - Wages shall be paid to the workers and employees working in the ship recycling industry in accordance with the minimum wage rate announced by the Ministry of Labour and Employment in the light of the recommendation of the Minimum Wage Board in accordance with the Bangladesh Labour Act 2006. (7) Every Ship Recycling Facility shall ensure strict compliance with all requirements prescribed in the Ship Recycling Facility Plan (SRFP) and the Ship Recycling Plan (SRP), which shall be prepared in full conformity with the applicable guidelines adopted by the Marine Environment Protection Committee (MEPC) through its resolutions and in accordance with the provisions set forth under Rule 8 and Rule 12, respectively.

(8) The Ship Recycling Facility shall be obligated to prepare and submit an annual report to the Board, along with any additional reports as may be directed, detailing its operational activities, accidents, and incidents at such intervals as may be prescribed by the Board.

(9) The ship recycling facility shall ensure safe and environmentally sound ship recycling by utilizing competent persons, environmental impact sufficient material handling equipment, personal protective equipment, and appropriate procedures as stipulated in the SRP and SRFP, and as prescribed by the Board.

(10) The Ship Recycling Facility shall, prior to importing a ship for recycling in Bangladesh, ensure that all cargo residues, bunker oil, and non-essential hazardous waste and materials onboard the ship—except those essential for maintaining the structural integrity of the vessel—are pre-cleaned to the maximum extent practicable. The extent of such pre-cleaning shall be determined based on the size, type, and capacity of the facility and shall be duly recorded in the Ship Recycling Plan (SRP).

(11) The ship recycling facility shall ensure that all tankers brought into Bangladesh for recycling have their cargo tanks and pump rooms ready for hot-work and man-entry certification as mandated in the SRP.

(12) The ship recycling facility shall have a management system, procedure, and technique, which do not pose health risks to the workers concerned and to the population in the vicinity of the ship recycling facility.

(13) The ship recycling facility shall make all reasonable and practicable efforts to employ full-time skilled workers or engage them through ship-specific worker’s representatives to ensure that training programs remain relevant, effective, and beneficial to the workers.

(14) Ship recyclers shall ensure that any clams of maritime lien attached to the ship are resolved as prescribed by the Board before the Ship is taken to the facility.

(15) Ship recyclers shall be mandated to maintain and preserve all relevant documentation in accordance with any recognized international standard, such as ISO 14001 or OHSAS 18001, ensuring that such records are readily available on-site for inspection by the Board.

(16) Ship recyclers and their representative trade association shall undertake all necessary measures to progressively promote and facilitate the participation of women in appropriate and relevant departments of ship recycling facilities (SRF).

(17) The SRF shall perform internal audits to verify compliance with the conditions outlined in the SRFP and SRP in a manner and at intervals specified by the Board.

**8. The Ship Recycling Facility Plan (SRFP): -** (1) The Ship Recycling Facility Plan (SRFP) shall be the principal governing document for each Ship Recycling Facility, and every Ship Recycling Facility shall prepare an SRFP in strict conformity with the structure prescribed in the guidelines adopted under the relevant resolution of the Marine Environment Protection Committee (MEPC), ensuring its sequential incorporation of all the sections and items as stipulated therein. Each Ship Recycling Facility shall ensure full compliance with all requirements prescribed in the SRFP and the Ship Recycling Plan (SRP). The issuance of a DASR shall be solely contingent upon the approval of the SRFP by the Ship Recycling Board (SRB) following this, Rule.

(2) The conformity of the Ship Recycling Facility Plan (SRFP) with the applicable Marine Environment Protection Committee (MEPC) guidelines, as referenced in sub-rule (1), shall be assessed objectively and be subject to the Board’s satisfaction. Any deviation from the prescribed structure, sequence, or content of the applicable guidelines shall require express authorization from the competent authority, granted on a case-by-case basis, considering the specific circumstances of the ship, the facility, and the recycling operations, while ensuring compliance with the applicable legal framework.

(3) The SRFP shall be adopted by the Board of the relevant governing body of the ship recycling company, which shall consistently comply with the policy adopted by its board.

(4) All clauses included in the SRFP shall include stipulated obligations which shall cover all the tasks described in the guidelines adopted by the relevant MEPC resolution as applicable as far as reasonable and practicable given the size and capacity of the Ship Recycling Facility and applicable ship recycling method. Failure to adhere to the conditions stipulated in the SRFP shall constitute a breach of the conditions of approval of SRFP by the Board regardless of whether an incident ensues from that breach.

(5) In preparing the SRFP the recycling owner shall demonstrate how they will comply with those conditions through the use of appropriate device, equipment, qualified personnel, and proper system or procedures.

(6) The SRFP shall include procedures for testing and inspecting ropes, chains, slings, hooks, chain-falls and hoisting and hauling equipment. It shall further include a description of operations using cranes, machines, mobile equipment and aerial and man-lift systems and a list of qualifications required for the operators as prescribed by the Board.

(7) The SRFP shall include procedures to ensure the appropriate level of certification and/or verification in order that all subcontractors (including those involved in handling, transport, treatment, storage and disposal) hold valid permits, registrations and/or certificates, adequate security as applicable

(8) The engagement of any subcontractor for the performance of activities involving the handling, management, or processing of Hazardous Materials, or any other ship recycling operations, shall in no manner absolve or discharge the Ship Recycling Facility from its statutory responsibilities, duties, or liabilities arising therefrom.

(9) The Board may promulgate comprehensive technical guidelines and a checklist of duties in accordance with the guidelines adopted by the relevant MEPC resolution as applicable to facilitate the preparation of the SRFP, ensuring its methodological, consistent and uniform implementation throughout all ship recycling facilities. The aforementioned checklist of duties may be utilized for audit purposes, both external and internal to assess the facility's adherence to the Hong Kong Convention and the guidelines adopted by the relevant MEPC resolution as applicable, and may be revised periodically as needed.

**CHAPTER IV**

**Ship recycling process**

**9. Import/procurement of scrap ships for recycling. -** (1) A No-objection certificate shall be obtained from the office of the Director General before importing or locally procuring ships for recycling. The SRF owner or his legal representative will submit an application in the prescribed form to the Director General for a no-objection certificate.

(2) To obtain a NOC, the following documents shall be included to the application form.:

(i) A copy of a valid Document of Authorization to Ship Recycle (DASR) or a provisional DASR except where an existing Ship Recycling Facility falls within the grace period as prescribed under sub-rules (15), (16), and (17) of Rule 7.

(ii) an updated environmental clearance certificate of the SRF from the Department of Environment;

(iii) A No Objection Certificate (NOC) for the importation of scrap ships from the Department of Environment

(iv) Memorandum of Agreement on the Sale of Ships, Bill of Sale, etc.;

(v) Certificate of registration of the vessel

(vi) Updated inventory of hazardous materials

(vii) A declaration by the owner of the SRF that the hazardous waste of the ship shall be managed in accordance with the existing Rules;

(viii) a money receipt for depositing the amount determined by the Government in favour of the Board in respect of the No Objection Certificate;

(ix) such other documents as may be prescribed from time to time by the Ministry of Industries;

(x) Updated membership certificate of the concerned trade association.

(xi) Any other documents that the Board considers fit and proper.

(3) The Director General shall dispose of the application within 2 (two) working days of receipt of the No Objection Certificate.

(4) After obtaining the No Objection Certificate from the Board, the SRF owner shall pay the price to the ship owner or his legal representative by making a Letter of Credit. Without a no-objection certificate from the office of the Director General, no bank shall pay the price in favour of any ship owner by letter of credit or any other method.

**10.  Inspection. -**

(1) Every Ship intended to be recycled in a ship recycling facility shall, at least three days prior to its arrival at Bangladesh Maritime zone notify the Maritime Rescue Coordination Centre (MRCC) or the Port Authority its intended date of arrival and such notification shall contain following particulars, namely: —

(a) Name and Address of the identified ship recycling facility; (b) the coordinates of the area;(c) estimated duration of stay at the anchorage; and (d) declaration that the ship is on its final voyage heading for recycling.

(2) The ship recycling facility shall, at least three days prior to the arrival of ship to Bangladesh maritime zone inform the Board about the ship’s arrival.

(3) The Board shall maintain a record of the information received under sub-rule (2) and the nature and particulars of the ships intended to be recycled in a ship recycling facility and on receipt of the expected time of arrival of such ships, shall immediately provide such record to Bangladesh Navy and the Coast Guard.

(4) Prior to the expected arrival of the ship at the anchorage, the ship recycling facility shall submit such documents and particulars in a manner, as may be required under the Customs Act, 2023 (57 of 2023).

(5) after the ship arrives at the port, the SRF owner or his representative shall inform the Director General of the Board and submit an application in the prescribed form for inspection of the ship.

(6) The application for inspection shall be accompanied by a no-objection certificate issued by the Board for the import of the vessel and a receipt of the deposit in favour of the Board for the inspection fee as fixed by the Government.

(7) The Director General of the Board shall issue an inspection order within 2 (two) working days of the receipt of the application for inspection, subject to verification of the correctness of the application. In order to complete the inspection process in the outer anchorages in the shortest possible time, the representative of the Board or the Safety Agency responsible in this regard and the designated officials of Bangladesh Customs, Department of Environment, Department of Explosives will complete the onboard inspection program in a team as prescribed. If for any reason a single visit is not possible, a separate visit will be made. The ship importing / procuring SRF authorities shall provide suitable safe vessels for inspection. The inspection shall be conducted in accordance with the applicable circulars issued by the relevant competent authorities, as prescribed therein, and shall encompass the following actions:

(8) Inspection of Customs Matters. -The Customs shall determine the duty of the ship and the attached / outfitting goods on the basis of the weight / LDT of the ship and issue the rummaging certificate in this regard;

(9) An Inspector of Explosives from the Directorate of Explosives shall inspect each tank and enclosed space using a calibrated and valid multi-gas detector, and certify that the space is free from petroleum and toxic vapors and is safe for entry and hot work. Where necessary, appropriate vapor testing equipment shall also be used to detect specific hazardous vapors, including VOCs and hydrocarbon residues, in accordance with applicable MEPC guidelines and standards

(10) The designated official of the Directorate of Explosives shall collect a comprehensive list of all types of cylinders, pressure vessels, and flammable or hazardous substances that pose a risk of explosion during handling. This list shall be provided to the Department of Explosives at the time of boarding at the outer

(11) For War Ships, Naval Ships, Nuclear Powered Vessels and large passenger ships representatives of the Bangladesh Atomic Energy Commission to verify the submissions/data provided at the time of desk review, during physical verification. If considered necessary, an adequate representative sample may be taken and used for the verification. Bangladesh Atomic Energy Commission shall examine such ships and accord their clearances or otherwise for beaching. To import said type of ships, adequate infrastructure at the yard to handle the identified quantities of radio-active materials and other hazardous wastes with adequate approved infrastructure of disposal facilities nearby, adequately trained staff, strict monitoring by Bangladesh Atomic Energy Commission, Department of Environment, Ship Building and Ship Recycling Board (SBSRB) is required.

(12) After the inspection of the goods and waste transported on the ship as far as possible and the report on the amount of hazardous waste updated on the ship (Inventory of Hazardous Waste Report), the report / certificate will be issued in the light of environmental laws, rules and circulars.

(13) The Bangladesh Navy shall send a report to the Board as soon as possible after inspecting the imported ship at the port / SRF for recycling purposes and identifying the radio communication equipment, pen pistol, line throwing equipment and other equipment which may destabilize the security of the country in accordance with the Rules. Only that equipment which are a threat to national security will be handed over to the Bangladesh Navy by the ship importing company / SRF owner. The equipment to be handed over to the Bangladesh Navy shall be duty free.

(14) After the above procedure, Inward General Manifesto is to be filed duly signed by the Master of the ship. Seller will pay to the Customs dues as applicable at the prevailing rate. Seller/agent is to submit arrival report (location of anchorage, time of arrival etc.) to Customs Authority

(15) Buyer/importer is to file bill of entry for home consumption with necessary documents for assessment of duty as prescribed by the Board and other relevant authorities in this regard;

**11.  Taking Ships to the Facility: -** (1) The Recycling Company or its representative shall, after completion of the inspection at the outer anchorage, submit an application to the Director General for permission to beach the ship in the form found in Annexure……..of this Rule. Every application shall be accompanied by the following documents, duly issued by the competent government authorities, containing the information as prescribed by the Board:

(i) Rummaging certificate

(ii) Gas free test certificate for safe for men entry and safe for hot work and safe dangerous petroleum vapour

(iii) A Ballast Water Exchange Certificate, issued by the Master of the vessel in compliance with the applicable national laws governing this matter.

(v) the inspection report of the Safety Agency in favour of the ship concerned;

(vi) Receipt of the deposit of the amount fixed by the Government in favour of the Board for the beach fees.

(vii) Trade Body’s valid membership document

(viii) The Director General of the Board shall dispose of the application for beaching within 2 (two) working days from the receipt of the application, subject to all the certificates submitted along with the application for beaching found correct.

(2) Upon obtaining permission to take the ship to the recycling facility, if it is subsequently discovered that the said ship has not been recycled and is instead operating as a cargo vessel, whether domestically or for ocean-going purposes, such an act shall constitute a criminal offense. The SRF owner or the company to whom the beaching permission was granted shall be held liable in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.

**12. Ship Recycling Plan (SRP):** (1) No Ship Recycler shall bring any ship to the recycling facility and/or undertake the recycling of any vessel without a ship-specific Ship Recycling Plan (SRP) duly approved by the Board and formulated in strict conformity with the guidelines adopted by the relevant MEPC resolution as applicable and the directives issued by the Board under sub-section (3).

(2) SRP shall be developed by the Ship Recyclers utilizing information from the Inventory of Hazardous Materials (IHM) prepared by the ship and approved by the ship's administration or another designated organization as prescribed by the board.

(3) The Board may, at its discretion, entrust the audit of the Ship Recycling Plan (SRP) submitted by Ship Recyclers for approval to a recognized organization or a specialist technical committee constituted for this purpose. The Board shall retain ultimate authority over SRP approval and may amend the plan, provided such amendments align with the Convention and the guidelines adopted by the guidelines adopted by the relevant MEPC resolution as applicable.

(4) The Ship Recycling Facility shall conduct the recycling process in accordance with the ship recycling phases prescribed by the Convention, adhering to the guidelines adopted by the relevant MEPC resolution as applicable and any directives issued by the Board.

(5) The shipowner's ship-specific information shall be assessed against the Ship Recycling Plan (SRP) to Conduct Ship Recycling's capabilities and restrictions. The SRP shall also address ship-specific issues not covered in the SRFP or that need separate arrangement. The SRP shall contain the following details:

(i) detailed information on the construction, ownership, type, repair, etc. of the vessel;

(ii) the ship's inventory of hazardous materials (IHM) and updated by a competent organization as prescribed by the Board;

(iii) the schedule of the recycling;

(iv) the detailed recycling process;

(v) details of the disposal and management of hazardous waste at the facility and disposal arrangement outside the facility;

(vi) the management of PPE for inspection onboard the ship;

(vii) List of workers employed in ship recycling.

(6) The Board may specify the guidelines for the preparation of a ship recycling plan for different categories of ships.

(7) Where a ship is to be recycled at more than one Ship Recycling Facility, the SRP shall specify the name and location of each Ship Recycling Facility, the specific activities to be undertaken at each facility, and the sequence in which such activities shall be carried out.

(8) The Board shall issue a written acknowledgment of receipt of the Ship Recycling Plan to the Ship Recycling Facility, the Ship Owner, and the Administration within three (3) working days from the date of its submission. The acknowledgment shall also specify the date on which the review period shall conclude.

(9) The Board may, upon affording the Ship Recycler an opportunity to be heard, refuse to grant approval of the Ship Recycling Plan (SRP) if it has reasonable grounds to believe that the SRP does not conform to the guidelines prescribed by the Board. In such an event, the Board shall, within fourteen days from the date of receipt of the acknowledgment of submission of the SRP for approval, communicate its objections in writing to the Ship Recycling Facility, the Shipowner, and the Administration.

(10) Where the Board fails to convey its decision regarding approval of the SRP within fourteen days of its submission, the plan shall be deemed to have been tacitly approved.

(11) Upon approval of the Ship Recycling Plan (SRP) by the Board, the Ship Recycling Facility shall transmit the approved SRP and relevant documents, as per applicable MEPC guidelines, to the shipowner. The shipowner shall submit the SRP to the ship’s Administration, which, upon satisfaction, shall issue the International Ready for Recycling Certificate (IRRC) in accordance with the Hong Kong Convention.

13. Cutting/recycling Permission of ships. –

(1) No ship shall be recycled without written permission or, as the case may be the tacit permission of the Boad obtained in such a manner as prescribed in this Rule.

(2) The owner of the SRF or his representative shall, after the ship is taken to the facility, submit an application to the Director General for permission for cutting/recycling the ship in the prescribed form. Along with the application, the following documents must be submitted:

(i) an approved SRP;

(ii) the updated list of workers employed in the SRF;

(iii) Gas-free certificate (safe for man-entry and safe for hot-work);

(iv) Inspection report of the Bangladesh Navy / List of seized goods;

(v) the No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Department of the Environment;

(vi) Certification by the Safety Agency or Board representative that, to the maximum extent practicable and in accordance with the Ship Recycling Plan (SRP), all waste, including operationally generated waste, hazardous materials in ship stores, and hazardous waste not part of the ship’s inbuilt structure, have been removed. Additionally, all ship tanks shall be thoroughly cleaned of oil, hazardous substances, residues, and flammable materials in compliance with the Hong Kong International Convention and the relevant guidelines of the Marine Environment Protection Committee (MEPC).

(vii) Receipt of fee deposit at the rate of the amount fixed by the Board per ship weight / LDT in favour of the Board for recycling or cutting fee;

(viii) A Ready for Recycling Certificate (IRRC) prepared in accordance with the HKC and its guidelines as adopted and issued by the ship’s Administration.

(3) After receiving the application, a representative of the Board will inspect the ship. During the inspection, all tanks and enclosed spaces of the ship will be re-examined on the ground and the Board will dispose of the application subject to ensuring safe for men entry and safe for hot work during the inspection. The Board may requisition services of representatives for inspection of such agencies as may be prescribed.

(4) The Director-General shall process and dispose of the application for ship recycling or cutting within seven working days from the date of receipt, subject to an onsite inspection. Upon approval, the Director-General shall specify the timeframe within which the recycling operation must commence. Failure to initiate the cutting operation within the prescribed timeframe shall render the cutting permit null and void, necessitating the recycling company to submit a fresh application to the Board, unless an extension is granted in accordance with the provisions of the relevant rules.

(5) If the Board fails to communicate its decision on the grant of cutting permission within seven working days from the date of receipt of the application, the cutting permission shall be deemed to have been granted by way of tacit approval. In such a case, the Ship Recycler shall submit a report to the Board, notifying the planned start of recycling of ships, in accordance with the reporting format prescribed by the Board.

(6) The Board may deny permission for recycling for reasons to be recorded in writing after affording an opportunity of being heard to the ship owner.

(7) While granting permission for recycling of ships, the Director General may fix the time for the recycling of ships as follows

(i) 10 (ten) to 30 (thirty) days for labour assembly and other preparatory work, depending on the size of the ship;

(ii) 15 days for one thousand LDT per ship;

(iii) an additional 60 days for tankers, barges or ships of special design;

(8) Due to natural calamities or special circumstances, the Director General may, on the application of the SRF Authority, extend the time allowed under sub-rule (4) up to a maximum of 3 (three) months.

(9) The Ship Recycler, on receipt of a copy of permission to recycle the ship, shall issue a statement of acceptance to the ship owner under intimation to the Board in such form and manner as may be specified by the regulations and thereafter the ship owner may get the ship de-registered.

(10) In case of failure to complete the recycling work within the permitted time or within the extended time, as the case may be, a penalty may be imposed at the rate of 5% of the recycling fee applicable to the ship concerned for each day.

(11) Services may be facilitated by introducing electronic method or online method or any other suitable method to facilitate the administrative work / approval process related to ship recycling. The Board may take necessary action in this regard. As part of the online service, online payment of government fees may be arranged.

(12) The Board shall have the authority to order the cessation or prohibition of ship purchases or imports by any individual, corporation, or organization from entities that have been sanctioned or blacklisted by the Board.

(13) The Board shall provide for online application system for obtaining various administrative approvals and may amend them time to time. Links for online application and for attachment of supporting documents shall be available on the Board's website. The Board shall take necessary steps to process the online applications electronically.

**CHAPTER V**

**Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)**

**14.** The Ship Recycling Facility shall have the following obligations related to OHS to be discharged in such a manner, specification and standard as prescribed in the SRFP:

(1) Ship recyclers shall ensure that key personnel at the Ship Recycling Facility have the appropriate knowledge, skills and experience for the intended job function

(2) The Ship Recycling Facility shall adopt an environmental, workplace safety, and health management system in accordance with recognized international standards as prescribed by the Board.

(3) The Ship Recycling Facility shall provide training programs that are adequate, effective and enable workers to safely perform all the assigned tasks and as stipulated in the SRFP.

(4) The Ship Recycling Facility shall maintain all vital records related to its activities, particularly the recycling of each ship, including adopted safety and environmental measures, and shall preserve all records for a minimum of five years for on-site inspection.

(5) The SRFP shall include a clear and concise description of the detailed facility information

(6) The SRF shall comply with all licensing requirements, permits, and certifications as mandated by national law.

(7) The vessel must be adequately secured upon arrival at the facility to prevent any damage from weather-related incidents.

(8) SRF shall assess the risks associated with managing a particular activity or waste by a team comprising the Competent Person, a management representative, and worker’s representatives possessing the requisite competence to mitigate and eliminate any unexpected occurrences.

(9) SRFP shall have procedures in place for recording and reporting any incidents and accidents

(10) The SRF shall ensure the safety and health of workers in the facility by complying with the Labour Law and all pertinent health and safety regulations established in domestic laws together with those stipulated in the SRFP.

(11) The SRF shall employ a sufficient number of full time Competent Persons and health management professionals, with a hierarchical structure if required, and ensure their constant present at the SRF to efficiently maintain safe operational conditions throughout ship recycling activities.

(12) SRF shall maintain Safe-for-hot-work and Safe-for-entry conditions and procedures throughout the ship recycling process in accordance with the specifications outlined in the SRFP, utilizing a competent person as designated by the Board to prevent any incidents at the facility.

(13) The SRF shall comply with the safe procedures for the handling, transportation, and storage of drums and containers containing hazardous compounds, as well as pressure vessels, including those utilized in firefighting, as outlined in the SRFP.

(14) The SRF shall establish appropriate procedures to prevent falls from heights and accidents resulting from falling objects.

(15) The SRF shall establish appropriate procedures for the testing, utilization, and inspection of gear and equipment for rigging and materials handling.

(16) The SRF shall establish appropriate protocols for work spaces, including aisles, corridors, and temporary deck openings, ensuring proper housekeeping and sufficient illumination where required.

(17)  The SRF shall provide appropriate changing rooms and sanitary and washing facilities to control exposure and avoid the spread of Hazardous Materials.

(18) The SRF shall implement appropriate procedures and use of personal protective equipment (PPE) in accordance with national law to safeguard employees from different dangers connected with ship recycling. The SRF must use the best industry practices globally available to ensure advanced and effective respiratory protection and hearing conservation programs.

(19) The SRF shall implement systems for monitoring exposure, advanced medical surveillance system, and early diagnosis of surreptitious medical conditions, such as asbestosis.

(20) Shipbreaking workers shall receive mandatory training on asbestos safety, including removal, containment, and disposal. All personnel shall use prescribed protective equipment, including respirators, coveralls, gloves, and decontamination units.

(21) Asbestos removal shall be conducted only within designated zones, sealed off from the general workforce and equipped with negative air pressure systems to contain airborne fibers. Access to these zones shall be restricted to certified asbestos removal personnel. The handling of asbestos-containing materials shall be strictly controlled, and the breaking, sanding, grinding, or any dry removal of such materials is prohibited. All asbestos removal shall be carried out using wet removal techniques or encapsulation procedures to prevent fiber release into the environment.

(22) The SRF shall maintain comprehensive health records and conduct mandatory annual medical examinations for all workers exposed to asbestos. Workers diagnosed with asbestos-related illnesses shall be entitled to compensation from the Workers’ Asbestos Protection Fund, which shall be contributed by all authorized ship recycling facility owners and administered by their trade association. The concerned ship recyclers shall also be responsible for providing adequate financial and medical assistance to asbestos-affected workers in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

(23) SRFs violating provisions on asbestos inventory, pre-cleaning, worker training, protective equipment, restricted handling, or medical surveillance shall be subject to penalties, including fines, suspension, or revocation of authorization. The Competent Authority shall conduct regular inspections to ensure compliance, and non-compliant facilities shall be required to take corrective action within a specified timeframe, failing which their authorization may be permanently withdrawn.

(24) All asbestos, including usable asbestos-containing material sheets or panels and broken pieces thereof, shall be securely packed in leak-proof containers and disposed of in secured landfills in accordance with the guidelines prescribed by the Department of Environment. The handling and processing of such materials at the landfill shall comply with the specified regulatory standards. The sale or resale of asbestos and asbestos-containing materials in any form is strictly prohibited.

(25) All boilers and pressure vessels recovered from ships designated for recycling shall be mandatorily destroyed and shall not be reused, sold, or transferred for any secondary use within or outside Bangladesh. The destruction process shall be carried out in a manner that ensures the boiler is rendered permanently inoperable.

(26) The ship recycling facility shall conduct the destruction under the supervision of the Office of the Chief Inspector of Boilers and other relevant regulatory authorities. The process shall comply with applicable laws, including the Environment Conservation Rules, 2023, and the Bangladesh Ship Recycling Act, 2018. The facility shall obtain a certificate of destruction upon completion of the process.

(27) The SRF shall formulate and uphold a comprehensive Emergency Preparedness and Response Plan (EPRP) in strict conformity with all applicable national laws and globally recognized industry best practices, ensuring an effective and coordinated emergency response. The EPRP shall constitute a self-contained document, maintained at a designated location within the facility for immediate accessibility, while simultaneously forming an integral part of the SRFP.

(28) Depending on the size and type of he Ship Recycling Facility, the ship recycling facility shall have permanent firefighting system installed with adequate capacity to prevent large scale fires and explosion, as described in the SRFP.

(29) Adequate and secure means of access and egress shall be provided at all workplaces throughout all stages of ship recycling operations and shall be maintained in a safe and operational condition at all times.

(30) To combat fire quickly and efficiently and limiting any outbreaks and ensuring safe evacuation of all of its personnel, ship breaking facility shall have procedure in pace to regularly carry out fire-drill and drill on preventing oil spillage.

**CHAPTER VI**

**Environmentally Sound Management**

**15. Provisions regarding environmentally sound management:** (1) The SRF shall manage and dispose of waste and hazardous waste in an environmentally sound manner implementing the relevant applicable international and national laws, regulations and guidelines notified by the Board in such a manner, specification and standard as prescribed in the SRFP.

(2). The SRF shall implement an environmental monitoring programme aimed at preventing possible negative impacts to the environment during ship recycling.

(3) Given the ship's size, type, and the ship recycling facility's capacity as indicated in the SRP, the SRF shall ensure that ships that are intended for recycling operate in a way that minimizes the amount of waste, fuel oil, and cargo residues left onboard as much as is practically possible before entering the ship recycling facility.

(4) To facilitate the identification of Hazardous Materials during the recycling process or in preparation for it, adequate precautions shall be implemented to ensure the safety of the sampler. A proper sampling plan should be developed, and samples must be analysed by an accredited laboratory.

(5) The Ship Recycling Facility shall utilize the information contained in the IHM to identify the type, location, and quantity of any Hazardous Materials and to designate and/or label them in a manner that is easily identifiable to the personnel involved in recycling.

(6) The Hazardous Materials that have been identified on the ship shall be safely removed, handled, and/or cleaned by the Ship Recycling Facility using appropriately trained personnel in accordance with the mandatory worker-safety measures for the Hazardous Materials in question.

(7) To prevent waste from contaminating recyclable materials and equipment at the facility post-removal, appropriate precautionary measures shall be implemented during processing, storage, treatment, recycling, or disposal.

(8) All hazardous waste generated at the Ship Recycling Facility shall be handled in an environmentally sound manner. Hazardous waste removed from the ship shall be sent to a state-authorized facility only for final disposal and shall be monitored until it arrives at its destination.

(9) When handling hazardous materials such as asbestos, polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs), ozone-depleting substances (ODSs), organotin compounds including tributyltin (TBT), and Cybutryne toxic and highly flammable paints, hazardous liquids, residues and sediments, as well as heavy metals like lead, mercury, cadmium, and hexavalent chromium, appropriate protective measures specific to each type of waste must be implemented as stipulated in the SRFP.

(10) The ship recycling facility shall implement methods and programs sufficient for spill prevention, response, and countermeasure tasks.

(11) Management of Hazardous Materials and wastes, recycled materials, reusable items shall be handled and/or disposed of in a safe and environmentally sound manner.

(12) SRF shall transfer hazardous waste only to the facilities authorized to handle their treatment and/or disposal in an environmentally responsible manner.

(13) The SRF shall include a program that delineates steps to be followed and sustained to mitigate the risk of storm-water pollution at the Ship Recycling Facility.

(14) The SRF shall have a programme that defines measures to be implemented and maintained to minimize the potential for debris deposition into the water.

(15) The SRF shall have detailed programme as describe the procedures for reporting incidents and spills occurred in that facility.

(16) The Director General may issue guidelines / instructions regarding the temporary storage and management of waste in the SRF until the opening of the Treatment, Storage and Disposal Facility (TSDF) of the Ministry of Industries / Board or before the waste is sent to the TSDF for final management.

(17) The Director General may, until the commencement of the TSDF with the approval of the Board, register or license the appropriate vendors for the management of waste of a particular nature and issue guidelines / instructions in this regard.

(18) The Director General may collect from the vendors the licence fee and other fees for the registration of vendors at the rate determined by the Board.

(19) The Director General may, subject to the approval of the Board, grant permission to dispatch the waste of a particular nature to an appropriate domestic or foreign institution in accordance with the applicable national and international laws and conventions in response to the application of the SRF owner for the management of waste until the TSDF is launched.

(20) The Director General may, subject to the approval of the Board, appoint a vendor in the light of sub-rule (4) for the management of waste of a particular nature even after the commencement of the TSDF and grant permission to send the waste to domestic or foreign institutions.

(21) In the operation of a ship breaking or cutting operation, such method shall be applied so that the oil block of the ship being re-processed does not come in contact with water or in any other way cause pollution of water, soil or air by oil or oil-like substances from the ship or any part thereof or by any other waste carried on the ship.

(22) Suitable cranes and floating platforms should be used to move the parts of the recycled ship, especially the oil block, to the paved area of the SRF, or similar arrangements should be made so that the oil block does not come in contact with the water or soil in the intertidal zone.

(23) The impermeable floor must be used to redistribute the oil block of the recycled vessel. The platform or floor should have a proper water drainage and treatment system so that the water or soil is not polluted by oil, oily substances or any impurities. Recycled water needs to be recycled. In addition, there should be arrangements for storing or processing oil or oil-like substances collected from water in the SRF in an environmentally friendly manner.

**CHAPTER VII**

**Ship Recycling Board (SRB)**

**16. Director General.** – The Director-General, on behalf of the Board, shall perform the functions and exercise the powers prescribed in section 13 of the Act and shall include the following matters:

(1) the overall supervision of ship recycling operations;

(2) the approval of the preliminary facility plans for ship recycling and the approval of the ship recycling plan;

(3) matters relating to liaison and cooperation with national and international organizations / organizations in ship recycling activities;

(4) Granting of No Objection Certificate (NOC) for the import or local procurement of ships for recycling, upon examination of ship details, Inventory of Hazardous Materials *,* and the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU), in compliance with the Import Policy Order of Bangladesh, to facilitate inspection, integration, and segregation of imported vessels.

(5) to take the necessary measures to protect the environment, the safety of workers and the protection of occupational health; to take measures to prevent accidents, to take measures in the light of the law on accidents and to exercise all powers;

(6) to deal with all administrative and financial matters of the Board and all other functions as the Boad deems appropriate;

(7) to perform the duties of the Member-Secretary of the Bangladesh Ship recycling Board;

(8) to implement the decisions of the Bangladesh Ship Recycling Board;

(9) to perform other duties assigned by the Government or the Board.

(10) Conduct regular inspections of the SRF, both announced and unannounced, and establish an independent auditing mechanism, encompassing both external and internal audits, to oversee working conditions, labour safety, environmental protection, and the appropriate implementation of the SRP and SRFP.

**CHAPTER VIII**

**Powers and functions of the Ship Recycling Board (SRB)**

**17.** (1) If the Board has reason to believe that an offence under this Act has been or is being committed at any ship recycling facility, such Authority or any officer authorised therefor in this behalf may, subject to the rules and regulations made under this Act, enter and search at all reasonable times with such assistance, if any, as such Authority or officer considers necessary, such ship recycling facility and examine any record, register, document, equipment or any other material object found therein and seize the same if such Authority or officer has reason to believe that it may furnish evidence of the commission of an offence punishable under this Act.

(2) The provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 relating to searches and seizures shall, so far as may be, apply to every search or seizure made under this Act.

(3) The Board may relax any requirement of these regulations for reasons to be specified in writing if it deems it necessary or expedient.

(4) If any question arises regarding the interpretation of any provision of these regulations, the decision of the Ministry of Industry shall be final, provided that such decision is supported by objective justification

**CHAPTER IX**

**Reporting requirements**

**18.** When a ship is recycled in accordance with the provisions of this Rule, a statement of completion containing such particulars as may be specified by the regulations shall be submitted by the Ship Recycler to the Board.

**19.** The Board shall give report to the Ministry of Industries, from time to time, which shall include information comprising the list of approved facilities, list of ships which have not complied with the provisions of this Act and action taken on such ships and list of ships recycled, as may be required by the Ministry of Industries.

**CHAPTER X**

**Accident response, penalties and worker’s compensation**

**20. Accidents response.** - (1) The SRF authority is required to promptly notify the Board, the Department of Inspection of Factories and Establishments, and the local police station in writing in the event of an accident, fire, or explosion in the SRF or ship.

(2) In the event of an accident, fire, or explosion that results in the death or grievous bodily injury of any individual while on the ship or in the SRF, or while in transit, the following measures shall be implemented:

(3) The SRF authority is required to conduct immediate rescue operations by temporarily suspending the SRF 's operations, administer appropriate treatment to the injured worker, and re-establish the safety risk.

(4) in the event of any accident / fire / explosion and in the event of the risk of re-spread of fire / explosion or in the event of a major security risk, the Director-General shall immediately declare all activities in the said SRF closed;

(5) In the occurrence of an accident, fire, or explosion, provided there is no risk of the fire or explosion reoccurring or posing a significant safety hazard, SRF operations may proceed in other secure areas of the SRF or vessel, excluding the accident site. However, the SRF authority shall ensure that all accident indicators are maintained in proper condition.

(6) The Director General shall mandate that an investigation into the accident be conducted within seven working days. The investigative team will comprise officials from the Board and the Directorate of Inspection of Factories and Establishments, together with experts possessing specialized knowledge in health management, marine engineering, environmental protection, and occupational health and safety.

(7) The investigation team shall perform an on-site inquiry, ascertain the cause of the accident, establish liability, and provide recommendation to prevent future occurrences.

(8) The Director General shall, based on the inquiry findings, implement requisite administrative measures against the individuals accountable as stipulated in the Bangladesh Ship Recycling Act 2018 and this Rule;

(9) The Director General shall direct the SRF authorities to implement appropriate steps within the designated timeframe, based on the recommendations of the investigative committee, to prevent future accidents

(10) Should the SRF 's operations be suspended owing to security concerns, the Director General shall evaluate the possibility of reopening the SRF after the implementation of appropriate steps by the SRF within the designated timeframe. If the cause of the accident is ascertained and necessary enhancements are made to the SRF 's capacity to eliminate such hazards, the resumption of SRF operations shall not be unduly delayed.

(11) The Board shall conduct a follow-up investigation to verify effectiveness of preventive measures against the accident and status of implementation of recommendations provided by the investigation team. Based on the result of the follow-up investigation as necessary the Board shall provide additional instructions and/or recommendations to ensure appropriate implementation of initial recommendation.

**21. Penalty. —Whoever contravenes any of the provision of these Rules be punishable in accordance with the provisions of section 25 or 47 of the Act. The aforesaid penal action shall be taken concurrently with other appropriate penal actions that may be taken under other relevant statutes, acts and regulations etc.**

**22. Worker’s Compensation:**

(1) In the event of a death resulting from an accident, fire, or explosion or howsoever occurring in the SRF, on the ship, or en-route, regardless of the cause, the SRF authority shall provide a compensation of 15 (fifteen) lakh taka to the lawful heirs of the deceased, in addition to the compensation stipulated in the Labour Act.

(2) In the event of a loss of limb of any employee or worker owing to serious accident, or in the case of permanent disability as determined by a licensed physician, the affected individual shall receive a sum 10 lakh takas in addition to any recourse available under the Labour Act 2006 and an amount equivalent to one year's salary and allowances/wages.

(3) In the event that an individual sustains any injury including fatal injuries from an accident, fire, or explosion or howsoever occurring in the SRF, on the ship, or en route, the SRF authority shall facilitate immediate and appropriate medical treatment and cover the expenses for immediate hospitalization and adequate medication until the individual is completely recovered.

(4) The SRF authority shall continue to pay regular salary, allowances and all other benefits in full to the injured individual, considering the duration of their illness as part of their service at the facility.

(5) The SRF owners shall treat all injured workers, regardless of the severity of their injuries or if they are deceased, equally. This shall apply to both regular and casual workers, whether directly appointed by the SRF owners or through contractor personnel, and irrespective of their rank or classification.

(6) No worker-employee engaged on a permanent basis shall be dismissed from service merely on account of being injured in an accident, nor shall the contract be terminated contrary to the consent of the worker in the case of a worker engaged on a ship specific contract or otherwise.

(7) The work experiences of casual workers employed at a facility affiliated with a registered trade body of ship recyclers in Bangladesh shall be counted for the purpose of claiming all job-related benefits stipulated in the BLA 2006, contingent upon the completion of a specified number of days or months or years of service at the workplace. The compensation will be disbursed from the funds established by the Shipbreaker association, with contributions from all SRF’s member to this association.

(8) It shall be the duty of the Ship Recycling Facility to ensure that only trained and certified workers are engaged in ship recycling operations and that such activities are conducted under continuous supervision in compliance with applicable laws and safety standards. Given the hazardous nature of ship recycling work, compensation payable to workers under this Rule shall not be reduced or withheld on the grounds of contributory negligence by the worker in cases of death or fatal injury sustained in the course of employment.

(9) The Director General may, with the prior approval of the Board, from time to time, re-determine the amount of compensation in case of death or serious injury of the person in charge or the worker-employee due to an accident in the SRF or in the recycling ship.

**CHAPTER XI**

**Review &amp; Appeal**

**23. Appeal: (1) Any Ship Recycler aggrieved by any order made or decision taken under these Rules by the Board or recognized organization, may prefer an appeal to the Secretary Ministry of Industries within a period of thirty days from the date of receipt of such order or decision: The Board may admit any an appeal after the expiry of the period aforesaid, if the appellant satisfies the Board that he had sufficient cause for not preferring the appeal on time**

(2) Any Ship Recycler aggrieved by any order made or decision taken under these Rules may prefer a review application before the office of the Secretary, Ministry of Industries within a period of thirty days from the date of receipt of such order or decision. Provided that the Secretary, Ministry of Industries may admit the review application after the expiry of the period aforesaid, if the appellant satisfies to the Secretary that he had sufficient cause for not preferring the review application on time.

(3) Every appeal/review preferred under this Rule shall be accompanied by a copy of the order appealed/reviewed against.

(4) No appeal preferred under this Rule shall be disposed without giving the appellant a reasonable opportunity of being heard.

(5) The Board shall be guided by the principles of natural justice and subject to other provisions of Ship Recycling Act and of any Rules made thereunder, shall have powers to regulate their own procedure, including the places at which it shall conduct its business for the purpose of any appeal preferred under this Rule.

(6) An appeal/review made under this Rule shall be disposed of as expeditiously as possible and the disposal of such appeal/review shall not exceed a period of three months from the date of filing the appeal/review.

(7) The Secretary, Ministry of Industries may confirm, modify or reverse the order appealed/reviewed against.

(8) Notwithstanding anything contained in any other law for the time being in force while exercising powers under this Rule, the Secretary while deciding any appeal/review under this Rule, shall have the same powers as are vested in a civil court under the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (5 of 1908), in respect of the following matters, namely: —

(i) the discovery and production of books of account and other documents, at such place and such time as may be specified by the Secretary, and examining them on oath;

(ii) inspection of any books, registers and other documents of any person;

(iii) issuing commissions for the examination of witnesses or documents;

(iv) subject to the provisions of sections 123 and 124 of the Evidence Act, 1872 (l of 1872),

requisitioning any public record or document or a copy of such record or document, from any office and production of such documents;

(v) receiving evidence on affidavits;

(vi) dismissing an application for default or deciding it, ex-parte;

(vii) setting aside any order of dismissal of any application for default or any order passed by it, ex-parte;

(viii) granting interim relief;

(ix) reviewing its decision; and

(x) any other matter as the Secretary may deem fit and proper.

(9) Every proceeding before the Secretary, Ministry of Industries shall be deemed to be a judicial proceeding within the meaning of sections 193 and 228, and for the purposes of section 196, of the Bangladesh Penal Code (45 of 1860) and shall be deemed to be a Civil Court for the purposes of section 195 and Chapter XXVI of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (2 of 1974)

**CHAPTER XIII**

**Repeal and removal**

**29.** (1) With the coming into force of these Rules, the Ship Breaking and Recycling Rules, 2011, hereinafter referred to as the 'repealed Rules', shall stand repealed.

(2) Notwithstanding such repeal, any act done or any action taken or any order, notification, notice, circular issued under the repealed Rules shall be deemed to have been done or taken or issued under this Rule.

**30. Translated into English.** - (1) After the commencement of these rules, the Government may, by notification in the official Gazette, publish an Authentic English Text translated into English of the Bengali text of these Rules.

(2) In case of a conflict between the Bengali text and the English text, the Bengali text shall prevail.
