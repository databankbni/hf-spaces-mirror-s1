**The Bangladesh Ship Recycling Act, 2018 (as amended 2025)**

(Act No. 8 of 2018)

**PREAMBLE**

An Act to regulate the recycling of ships by establishing specific standards and establishing the statutory mechanism for their enforcement, as well as for matters related to or incidental to the recycling process.

WHEREAS, the International Maritime Organization adopted the Hong Kong International Convention for the Safe and Environmentally Sound Recycling of Ships, 2009 which ensures that ships, when being recycled after the end of their operational lives, do not pose any unnecessary risk to the environment and to human health and safety;

AND WHEREAS, the said Convention was developed with inputs from International Maritime Organization Member States, Non-Governmental Organizations and in co-operation with the International Labour Organization and the Parties to the Basel Convention on the Control of Transboundary Movements of Hazardous Wastes and their Disposal, 1989;

AND WHEREAS, the said Convention contains provisions which are not covered in the Ship Breaking and Ship Recycling Rule 2011 notified earlier by the Government to regulate the recycling of ships in Bangladesh;

WHEREAS the said Convention designates the Party responsible for regulating ship recycling activities to the jurisdiction under which the ship recycling facility operates, as established principally by the conditions outlined in the authorization document granted for such activities;

AND WHEREAS, Bangladesh, being a Member-State of the International Maritime Organization, had acceded to and participated in the said Convention and expressed its commitment for the protection of environment and human health and safety during the process of recycling of ships;

Whereas it is expedient and necessary to make provisions for the overall development, operation and growth of the ship recycling industry; Accordingly, it was enacted as follows:

**CHAPTER II**

**Initial**

**1. Short title and introduction** . (1) This Act may be called the Bangladesh Ship Recycling Act, 2018.

(2) This Act shall come into force at once.

**2. Definition- In this Act, unless there is anything repugnant in the subject or context,**

1. "no-objection certificate" means a no-objection certificate issued by the Board under sub-section (2) of section 6;"
2. "permission" means the permission granted by the Government under sub-section (1) of section 5
3. "yard" "means any land in the zone allotted for the use of ship recycling;"
4. "Chairman" "means the Chairman of the Board;"
5. "clearance" "means the clearance granted by the Board under sub-section (5) of section 6;"
6. "ship recycling " "means the removal and management of disassembled and disassembled parts of a ship;"
7. "Ship Recycling Plan" means a plan formulated in accordance with the prescribed guidelines or rules for ship recycling, to be prepared in both English and Bengali languages.
8. "ship recycling facility plan" "means a plan for the use of yard or other facilities for ship recycling;"
9. "zone" "means any zone declared by the Government under section 4;"
10. "Fund" "means the Fund of the Board;"
11. "regulation" "means a regulation made under the Act;"
12. "Code of Criminal Procedure" "means the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898 (Act No." V of 1898).
13. "rules" "means rules made under this Act;"
14. "Board" "means the Bangladesh Ship Recycling Board established under section 8;"
15. "Director-General" "means the Director-General of the Board;"
16. "Member" "means a member of the Board; and"
17. "beaching" "means bringing a ship to the seabed of a yard for the purpose of recycling."

**3. Authorities under the Act-**

(1). The Government hereby appoints the ‘Bangladesh Ship Recycling Board’ as the ‘Competent Authority’, to execute duties related to ship recycling facilities operating within the territories of Bangladesh as determined.

(2). The Competent Authority shall identify a single contact point in the rank of Joint Secretary to act as central communicating partner between the Competent Authority, Administrations and Ship Recycling Facilities.

(3) Notwithstanding anything contained in any other law for the time being in force, the provisions of this Act shall have effect as the governing law in respect of matters relating to ship recycling. In the event of any inconsistency, the provisions of this Act shall prevail over the provisions of any other laws.

**CHAPTER II**

**Zone declaration, etc.**

**4. Zone declaration:** (1) The Government may, in such manner as may be prescribed by it, by notification in the official Gazette, declare and extend any suitable area as a zone for the purpose of confining ship recycling activities to a specified area.

(2) The Government may, for the purpose of declaring and extending the zone, acquire land and grant lease in such manner as may be prescribed by rules for establishing a yard therein.

**5. Setting up the yard -(1) Any person or institution may, with the permission of the Government, establish a yard in any zone**

(2) No yard shall be established or any similar establishment shall be constructed for the purpose in any area outside the zone.

(3) Permission shall be obtained, within the time prescribed by the Government, for the yards established within the declared zone before the coming into force of this Act.

(4) Unless permission is obtained under sub-section (3), the activities in the said yard shall not be carried on and the property situated therein shall be forfeited.

(5) The conditions, manner and fee for grant of permission to establish a yard under sub-section (1) shall be determined by the Board.

**6. Ship Recycling -(1) Vessels imported or locally collected in a yard may be recycled.**

(2) No-objection certificate of the Board shall be obtained before importing or locally procuring a ship under sub-section (1).

(3) After importing or, as the case may be, procuring any vessel under sub-section (1), an employee authorized by the Board shall inspect the vessel for grant of clearance for beaching and division.

(4) After inspection under sub-section (3), a ship shall be subjected to beaching, recycling and other ancillary activities.

(5) No ship-sailing, recycling and ancillary activities shall be carried out under sub-section (4) without obtaining clearance from the Board.

(6) Matters relating to grant of no-objection certificate, inspection, beach-building and ship recycling, grant of clearance and fee under this section shall be determined by rules.

(7) The Government and the yard owners may take necessary measures to introduce other environmentally friendly methods as an alternative to beach cleaning for ship recycling.

(8) The Board may, in accordance with the type and size of the vessel imported or collected for recycling, specify the period of division or cutting of the vessel in such manner as may be prescribed by rules.

(9) If the cutting of the ship is not completed within the period specified under the Rules referred to in sub-section (8), the Board may, upon objective assessment and being satisfied with the grounds, extend the deadline for completion of such cutting.
If the yard fails to comply with the extended deadline, the Board may impose a fine as may be prescribed by the Rules, after affording the yard an opportunity of being heard.

(10) All vendors involved in ship recycling shall obtain licence from the Board subject to payment of fee as prescribed by rules.

**7. The preservation of international standards-** (1) The Board shall ensure compliance with the international laws and conventions relating to the ship reprocessing industry in carrying out ship reprocessing activities.

(2) In order to comply with the conditions of The Hong Kong International Convention for the Safe and Environmentally Sound Recycling of Ships, 2009, the Government shall take effective measures to acquire the necessary capacity within a maximum period of 5 (five) years from the commencement of this Act.

(3) The Board may, for this purpose, prepare guidelines or, from time to time, issue directives in the light of the provisions of international law and conventions.

**CHAPTER III**

**Bangladesh Ship Recycling Board**

**8. Establishment of Board** : (1) After the commencement of this Act, the Government shall, as soon as may be, by notification in the Official Gazette, establish a Board to be called the Bangladesh Ship Recycling Board.

(2) The Board shall be a body corporate and shall have perpetual succession and a common seal and shall, subject to the provisions of this Act, have power to acquire, hold and transfer property, both movable and immovable, and the Board may sue and be sued in its own name.

**9. The Office of the Board- (1) The principal office of the Board shall be established in the divisional city of Chattogram.**

(2) The Board may, if necessary, with the prior approval of the Government, establish its branch office at any place in Bangladesh.

**10. Formation of the Board-** The Board shall consist of not less than twelve (12) and not more than sixteen (16) members, to be appointed by the Ministry of Industries in the following manner:

(a) Twelve members designated by virtue of their official positions among senior relevant Government officials, including— (i) One representatives from the Ministry of Industries holding the rank of no less than Additional Secretary who shall be the Chair of the Board, (ii)  Two representatives from the Ministry of Industries holding a rank of no less than Joint Secretary (iii) One representative from the Ministry of Shipping holding a rank of no less than Joint Secretary (iv) One representative from the Ministry of Environment &amp; Forest holding a rank of no less than Joint Secretary (v) one representative from the Ministry of Labour and Employment holding a rank of no less than Joint Secretary, (vi) One representative nominated by Bangladesh Navy , (vii) One representative from National Board  of Revenue, (viii) Divisional Commissioner of Chittagong, (ix) Deputy Inspector General, Chattogram Range, Bangladesh Police (x) A representative nominated by the Energy and Mineral Resources Division, not below the rank of Joint Secretary or equivalent (xi) Director General of Ship Breaking Board who also shall work as the member secretary of the Board

(b) The Board may, subject to the approval of the Ministry of Industries, co-opt one or more persons as members of the Board, if necessary.

(c) The Board shall, with the prior approval of the Government (Ministry of Industries), constitute a high-level Technical Committee comprising six (6) members, to be nominated by the Government on the basis of relevant qualifications and experience, for the purpose of advising the Board on policy formulation and decision-making in relation to technical matters, international legal instruments and industry best practices.

(d) The members of the Technical Committee shall be nominated by the Board and shall hold office for a term of three (3) years. However, the Board may, at its discretion, replace any member at any time, if deemed appropriate.

(e) The members as mentioned in subsection (c) above shall represent diverse interests of the ship recycling industry and shall possess proven expertise in relevant fields, including but not limited to naval architecture, maritime transport, maritime engineering, ship recycling trade and economics, maritime law and ship recycling regulatory frameworks.

(d) The Ministry of Industries shall assess and determine the suitability of the nominated members based on their qualifications and experience. The Board shall prescribe the terms of reference of the Technical Committee.

(e ) A person shall be disqualified from appointment or continuation as a member of the Board if he— (a) has been removed or dismissed from government service, local authority, or a government-owned or controlled corporation; (b) has been convicted and sentenced to imprisonment for an offence deemed by the Government to involve moral turpitude; (c) is an undischarged insolvent; or (d) has any direct or indirect share or interest in any work executed by order of the Board or in any contract or employment with, by, or on behalf of the Board.

(f ) Provided that no individual shall be considered to own a stake or interest in such contract, or employment solely due to his—

(i) possessing a stake in any company or firm that may contract with or be engaged by the Board; or (ii) possessing a stake or interest in any newspaper wherein advertisements pertaining to the Board's affairs may be published; or (iii) having an interest in any loan extended to the Board; or (iv) possessing a stake or interest in any lease, sale, exchange, or acquisition of immovable property or any agreement thereof; or (v) possessing a stake or interest in any lease or license granted by the Board or any right, by agreement or otherwise, to the exclusive or preferential use of any premises or property belonging to the Board; or (vi) possessing a stake or interest in the occasional sale to the Board, not exceeding ten thousand taka in any single financial year, of any object in which he engages in trade.

**11. Functions and Powers of the Board:** Subject to the other provisions of this Act, the functions and powers of the Board shall be as follows: -

(a) the overall supervision of ship recycling operations in Bangladesh as covered by regulation as promulgated by the Government;

(b) review and evaluate the overall activities of the Yard from time to time;

(c) the approval of the Ship Recycling Plan (SRP);

(d) the approval of the Ship Recycling Facility Plan (SRFP);

(e) to take action in the light of labour laws and petroleum and explosives laws to ensure the health and safety of workers and to eliminate risks in the workplace, and to coordinate with the relevant ministries, agencies or departments where necessary;

(f) to take action in the light of environmental law to ensure ship recycling in an environmentally sound manner and to coordinate with the relevant ministries, agencies or departments where necessary;

(g) to take action in the light of the existing laws relating to Bangladesh Customs and Excise and to coordinate with the relevant ministries, agencies or offices, if necessary;

(h) to send recommendations or proposals to the Government for the development of the ship recycling industry and to take action in accordance with the directives of the Government in this regard;

(i) to make recommendations to the Government on the establishment of new zones or the expansion of existing zones for the purpose of developing the ship recycling industry;

(j) Undertake national and international cooperation in ship recycling activities and for this purpose, with the prior approval of the Government, enter into agreements with domestic and foreign organizations or institutions;

(k) liaise with national and international organizations, research institutions and universities; and

(l) to exercise such other powers and perform such other functions as may be assigned to him by the Government.

**12. Meeting-(1) Subject to the other provisions of this section, the Board may determine the procedure of its meetings.**

(2) The meeting of the Board shall be held at the place and time fixed by the Chairman.

(3) At least one meeting of the Board shall be held every four (4) months.

(4) The Chairman shall preside at all meetings of the Board and in his absence one of its members nominated by the Chairman shall preside at the meeting.

(5) Every member of the Board shall have one vote and the decision of the meeting shall be taken by a majority vote, but in case of equality of votes, the person presiding over the meeting shall have the power to cast a second or casting vote.

(6) Attendance of not less than one-third of the total number of members of the Board shall be required to fill the quorum at a meeting of the Board, but in the case of an adjourned meeting no quorum shall be required.

**13. The Director General of the Board** : (1) The Government shall appoint an appropriate public servant as the Director General of the Board.

(2) The Director General shall be the Chief Executive of the Board and shall exercise the powers and perform the functions assigned to him by the Board.

(3) The Director-General shall be responsible to the Board for the performance of his functions and those of his subordinates.

the Employees of the Board

**14. the Employees of the Board:** (1) The Board may, in accordance with the organizational structure approved by the Government, appoint such number of employees as may be necessary for the discharge of its functions.

(2) The conditions of appointment and service of the employees of the Board shall be determined by regulations.

(3) For the purpose of this Act, the Government may requisition suitable employees from the Department of Environment, Bangladesh Customs, Directorate of Labour, Directorate of Inspection of Factories and Establishments and Directorate of Explosives.

**CHAPTER IV**

**Visit**

**15. visit- (1) Subject to the provisions of this section, any person generally or specially authorized by the Board in this behalf may inspect the yard, imported or locally procured vessels and ship recycling activities.**

(2) During the inspection under sub-section (1), the authority operating the yard and the ship importing company and all the officers and employees of the ship shall be obliged to provide necessary assistance.

**16. Power to enter, call for records, inquire, etc.** -For the purposes of this Act, every employee empowered in this behalf by the Board in general or in particular may exercise the following powers, namely: -

(a) to enter any yard, vessel or project area and to question any person or to ask for and review any records or data;

(b) inspecting the yard ship or anything located in the project area; and

(c) to conduct any search or sample collection or survey in the said yard, ship or project area.

**CHAPTER V**

**Environmental protection and workers' health and safety**

**17. Protection of the environment** – (1) For the purpose of this Act, the Government may issue guidelines or directions in the light of the existing laws and rules relating to the environment for the collection, storage and management of all types of waste produced from ship recycling activities.

(2) The Government shall construct a Treatment Storage and Disposal Facility (TSDF) for the management of waste generated from ship recycling activities within a period not exceeding 5 (five) years from the commencement of this Act.

(3) The use of TSDF shall be mandatory for all concerned and the Government may issue guidelines or instructions regarding its operation and use.

**18. Class of workers** (1) The Government may formulate classifications of work according to the type of ship recycling work by determining the minimum qualification.

**19. Training and placement of workers** - (1) The Board shall provide suitable training in the manner prescribed in the rules for the purpose of acquiring the necessary skills according to the work classification.

(2) In order to improve the skills of workers and employees, the Ministry of Industries shall establish a training institute within a period of not more than five (5) years from the commencement of this Act.

(3) The Board shall maintain a database of trained workers and employees and the yard owners shall recruit workers from among the workers included in the database.

**20. Insurance and compensation-** (1) The yard owners shall compulsorily insure life in the name of all workers and employees working in the yard.

(2) If any worker-employee dies or is seriously injured due to an accident in the yard, the yard owners may, in the manner prescribed by the rules, pay compensation to his family or to him in addition to the compensation mentioned in the Bangladesh Labour Act, according to the efficiency and work classification of the worker-employee.

**CHAPTER VI**

**Board Reporting and Financial Management**

**21. Annual Report-** (1) The Board shall, within three months after the end of the financial year, submit to the Government an annual report containing details of the activities carried out by it in each financial year.

(2) The Government may, if necessary, at any time call for statements, returns, reports and information from the Board on any of its matters and the Board shall be bound to furnish the same to the Government.

**22. The Funds of the Board-** (1) The Board shall have a fund and the following amount shall be deposited therein, namely: -

(a) loans and grants made by the Government;

(b) Grants received from any foreign government, organization or international organization with the prior approval of the Government;

(c) the income derived from the investment of the property of the Board;

(d) the fees collected by the Board, if any; and

(e) Money received from any other source approved by the Government.

(2) Necessary expenditure of the Board shall be met from the Board's fund in accordance with the budget approved by the Government.

(3) The amount of the fund shall be deposited in any scheduled bank approved by the Board.

(4) The money of the fund shall be invested in the sector approved by the Government.

(5) The accounts of the Fund shall be operated under the joint signature of the Director-General and another officer nominated by the Chairman.

(6) If, after meeting its expenditures for any financial year, there remains a surplus in the Fund of the Board, twenty-five percent (25%) of such surplus shall be deposited into the Government Treasury in accordance with the directions of the Government, and the remaining seventy-five percent (75%) may be retained as fixed deposit in a bank or may be otherwise invested.

**explanation** . - In this section, "Scheduled Bank" means the Bangladesh Bank Order, 1972 (P.O. "" "Scheduled Bank" "as defined in Article 2 (j) of No. 127 of 1972)."

Budget

**23. Budget** - The Board shall, within such time as may be prescribed by the Government each year, submit to the Government the annual budget statement for the next financial year and state therein the amount of money required from the Government for that financial year, subject to the expenditure to be met from the Board's funds.

**24. Accounting and auditing** – (1) The Board shall maintain the accounts of each financial year properly in the manner prescribed by the Government and shall prepare the annual financial statement of the accounts.

(2) The Comptroller and Auditor-General of Bangladesh, hereinafter referred to as the Auditor-General, shall audit the accounts of the Board every year and shall forward a copy of the audit report to the Government and the Board.

(3) In addition to the audit referred to in sub-section (2), the Bangladesh Chartered Accountants Order, 1973 (P.O. The accounts of the Board may be audited by a Chartered Accountant as defined in Article 2 (1) (b) of the Constitution (No. 2 of 1973) and for this purpose, the Board may appoint one or more Chartered Accountants and the Chartered Accountant so appointed shall be paid remuneration at such rate as may be determined by the Government.

(4) For the purpose of auditing the accounts of the Board, the Comptroller and Auditor General or any person authorized by him or a chartered accountant appointed under sub-section (3) may examine all the records, documents, annual balance sheet, cash or money deposited in the bank, collateral, reserves or other property, etc. of the Board and may question the Chairman, members or any members and employees of the Board.

**CHAPTER VII**

**Crime, Punishment and Prosecution**

**25. Measure and extent of penalty -** The Director General may order the suspension of all or a portion of the yard's activities for a specified period if the investigation demonstrates that the accident was the result of the yard authorities' negligence in fulfilling their responsibilities as outlined in the rule, SRP, or SRFP, and impose a financial penalty in any amount as prescribed in this Act that is reasonable in the circumstances, taking into account the nature of the incident or accident and the degree of negligence of the yard owner or its agent.

**26. Penalty for setting up a yard without permission -** Whoever establishes and/or operates a yard or attempts to do so outside the ship recycling zone declared by the board by this Act shall be subject to imprisonment for a term that may extend to two years, a fine that may extend to 30 lakh taka, or both.

**27. Penalty for ship recycling without an approved DASR** - Whoever establish and /or operates a yard without any DASR issued as per this rule and approved by the Board shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to two years or with fine which may extend to 30 lakh taka or with both.

**28. Penalty for ship recycling without an approved SRP** -Whoever undertakes ship recycling without an SRP issued in accordance with this regulation and approved by the Board shall be required to halt yard operations and may face imprisonment for a duration of up to two years, as well as a financial penalty ranging from 10 lacs to a maximum of 30 lacs, or both.

**29. Penalty for illegal import of ships for recycling** -Whoever imports or collects a vessel for recycling without a No Objection Certificate from the Board must return the vessel to the exporter at their own expense and may be subject to imprisonment for up to two years, along with a financial penalty between 10 lakh taka and 30 lakh taka or both. If the ship cannot be restored, the government may confiscate it and dispose of or sell it as it sees fit. All expenses whatsoever related to the ship and its disposal, if applicable, shall be incurred by the importer of the ship or its agent.

**30. Penalty for producing or submitting fake certificates or documents** -Whoever, at any point in the process of acquiring authorization to establish a yard or import vessels for recycling, or in obtaining a no-objection certificate for local collection, inspecting ships, securing clearance for beaching or recycling, or during any phase of recycling, presents or submits counterfeit certificates or documents to the Board or any of its authorized employee for fraudulent purposes shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to six month or with fine which shall be no less than Taka 5 (five) lac and no more than Taka 20 (twenty) lac.

**31** . **Penalty for beaching and recycling of vessels without No Objection Certificate -** Whoever brings a vessel to the ship recycling facility without No Objection Certificate from the Board may be subject to imprisonment for up to two years, along with a financial penalty between 20 lakh taka and 40 lakh takas, or both. The government may confiscate the ship and dispose of or sell it to anyone that it deems fit. All expenses whatsoever related to the ship and its disposal, if applicable, shall be incurred by the yard owner or its operator.

**32. Penalty for recycling of vessels without No Objection Certificate-** Whoever begins or attempt to begin ship recycling/cutting at the facility without any approval obtained from the Board shall be required to halt ship recycling and yard operations and may face imprisonment for a duration of up to one years, as well as a financial penalty ranging from 10 lacs to a maximum of 20 lacs, or both

**33. Penalty for violating the conditions stipulated in the SRP/SRFP-** Whoever contravenes any provisions related to occupational safety and health or environmentally sound management as prescribed in the rule and/or any conditions stipulated in the SRP or SRFP as approved by the Board shall be punishable with an imprisonment for a term which may extend to three months or with fine which may extend to five lakh taka or with both.

**34. Penalty for disobeying any order of the Board-** Whoever fails to respond to any orders or notifications sent to ship recyclers about required actions or remedial measures in accordance with their legal obligations shall be punishable—

(i) with a fine which may extend to three lakh takas in case of non-response within twelve hours of issuance of first notice;

(ii) with a fine which may extend to five lakh takas in case of non-response within twenty-four hours of issue of second notice; and

(iii) with an imprisonment which may extend to three months and with a fine which may extend to five lakh takas in case of non-response beyond twenty-four hours of issue of third notice. It is to be noted that if the matter concerns a serious safety and health issue that poses a probable and substantial risk to the life of any individual present on the premises, the yard owner shall be held fully liable for all resulting consequences. Depending on the degree of injury and the gravity of the offense, the yard owner shall be subject to a fine not exceeding ten (10) lakh taka, imprisonment for a term not exceeding one (1) year, or both.

**35. Penalty for appointing untrained workers at the facility** - The Board may impose a punishment of up to ten thousand taka per worker on the yard authority if untrained workers are involved in ship recycling activities. If an unskilled labourer is engaged by any vendor, the Director General may mandate a suspension of labour supply from that vendor for a specified duration.

**36. Penalty for appointing minor workers-** If any worker below the age of 18 years is engaged in ship recycling work, the Director General may impose a fine of up to 20 (twenty) thousand taka per worker on the yard authority.

**37. Penalty for not utilizing PPE at the yard -** The Director General may impose a fine of up to Taka 10 (ten) thousand per worker on the yard authorities if any worker is employed in ship recycling work without proper PPE.

**38. Recyclers' right to get notice and fair hearing before sanction** - If the Competent Authority is satisfied that there has been a breach of the provisions of this rule or the regulations made thereunder, it may, without prejudice to any criminal action that it may take against such Ship Recycler, suspend or cancel the authorization of his ship recycling facility: Provided that no such authorization shall be suspended or cancelled without giving an opportunity of being heard in the matter to the Ship Recycler

**39. Imposing penalties without prior notification in the interest of the public** - Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-rules (1) and (2), if the Competent Authority is of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient so to do in public interest, it may, for reasons to be recorded in writing, suspend or cancel the authorization of any ship recycling facility without issuing any notice referred to in sub-section (1).

**40. Offences committed by the company** -Notwithstanding the provisions of sub-section (1), if a company commits an offence under this Act, and it is demonstrated that the offence occurred with the consent, connivance, or neglect of any director, manager, secretary, or other officer of the company, such individuals shall also be considered culpable and subject to prosecution and punishment accordingly. Explanation. —For the purposes of this section, —

(a) “company” means anybody corporate and includes a co-operative society, firm or other association of individuals; and

(b) “director” means a whole-time director in the company and in relation to a firm means a partner in the firm.

**41. Offences committed by the company and individual responsibility** -In cases where a company has committed an offence under this Act, every individual who was in charge of and responsible for the company's business conduct at the time of the offence, along with the company itself, shall be considered guilty of the offence and subject to legal action and penalties accordingly: No one shall be subject to penalties under this Act if they can demonstrate that the offense occurred without their knowledge or that they exerted all reasonable care to avert the occurrence of the offense.

**42. Penalty when no specific punishment has been prescribed** - Whoever contravenes any of the provisions of this Act or any rules or regulations made thereunder, for which no specific punishment has been provided in this Act, may be mandated to cease yard operations and punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to three months or with fine which may, depending on the nature of violation, extend to two lakh taka or with both and, in the case of a continuing contravention, with an additional fine which may extend to five thousand taka for every day during which such contravention continues after the conviction for the first such contravention.

**43. Punishment for a second or repeat offense** -Whoever perpetrates a repeated offense shall incur a penalty or imprisonment or both that is double the amount of the previous sanction imposed upon him for the same offense.

**44. Board’s Power of Cancellation of permit and seizure of goods** -(1) If any person is found guilty of any of the offences mentioned in this Act, the Board may suspend or cancel the permission of the concerned yard in the manner prescribed by the Government.

(2) If any person is found guilty of an offence under this Act, the Court may confiscate the yard, ship and its goods.

**45. Board’s power to arrest-** If any person commits or attempts to commit any offence under this Act, the Board may lodge a complaint against him as well as confiscate the yard’s property, ship and its goods.

**46. Trial, bailability, non-enforceability and compoundability of offences-** (1) Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in any other law for the time being in force, offences under this Act shall be tried by a Judicial Magistrate of the first class or a Metropolitan Magistrate.

(2) Offences under this Act shall be compoundable, non-cognizable and non-bailable.

(3) The trial of any offence committed under this Act shall be held in a summary manner.

(4) Notwithstanding anything contained in the Code of Criminal Procedure; in imposing a fine on any person under this Act, the Court shall have power to impose such fine as may be specified in this Act.

Explanation. For the purposes of this section, -

(i)"Judicial Magistrate" "means a Judicial Magistrate as defined in section 11 of the Code of Criminal Procedure;"

(ii) Metropolitan Magistrate" "means a Metropolitan Magistrate as defined in section 18 of the Code of Criminal Procedure."

**47. Application of the Criminal Procedure Code** -(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Code of Criminal Procedure shall apply to the investigation, trial and appeal of any offence mentioned in this Act, and to detention and all other matters connected therewith.

(2)A case initiated on the basis of the report of the Director General or any employee authorized by him under this Act shall be deemed to be a case initiated on the basis of the police report under the Code of Criminal Procedure.

**CHAPTER VIII**

**Miscellaneous**

**48. One-stop service -** In order to complete the administrative work related to ship recycling properly and in the fastest possible time, the Board shall introduce an inter-ministerial one-stop service in the manner prescribed by the rules.

**49. Special provisions relating to emergency beaching of ships-** (1) In times of disaster, in the interest of safety, if it is necessary to beach a ship on an emergency basis, the ship may be beached with the permission of the local administration.

(2) If a ship is beached on an emergency basis under sub-section (1), the matter shall be immediately reported to the Board by the Director-General.

explanation. "For the purposes of this section," "local administration" "means the Deputy Commissioner of the district concerned or any officer authorized by him."

**50. Report submitted by the authority-** (1) Every Yard Authority shall submit a report to the Board every year containing details of the activities carried out by it by 31st March of the following year.

(2) The Board may, if necessary, at any time call for statements, returns and reports or any information on any of its matters from any Yard Authority and the Yard Authority shall be bound to furnish the same to the Board.

**51. The Committee** -The Board may, subject to such limits and conditions as may be prescribed by it, constitute one or more committees consisting of any member of the Board, any officer of the Board and, if necessary, any expert to assist it in the exercise of any of its powers or in the performance of any of its functions under this Act.

**52. Delegating power -** The Board may, by order in writing, delegate any of its powers to the Chairman, any member, any officer, committee or any person.

public servant

**53. Public servant -** The members and other employees nominated by the Government, while discharging their duties under this Act, shall be governed by the provisions of the Penal Code, 1860 (Act No. Section 21 of XLV of 1860) shall be deemed to be a Public Servant.

**54. The power to make laws-** (1) The Government may, by notification in the official Gazette, make rules for carrying out the purposes of this Act.

(2) Until rules are made under this Act, the Ship Breaking and Ship Recycling Rules-2011, hereinafter referred to as the said Rules, shall apply subject to conformity with this Act.

(3) Any case filed under the said Rules and any proceeding taken or initiated, if pending, shall be disposed of as if this Act had not been made.

**55. The power to make regulations-** For the purposes of section 14, the Board may, with the previous approval of the Government and by notification published in the official Gazette, make regulations consistent with this Act or the rules made thereunder.
