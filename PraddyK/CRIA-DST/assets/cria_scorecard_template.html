Biophysical asset scorecard — basin condition
Live from the VIC reanalysis + observations. Each sub-basin scored 0–100 on the validated ASI signals — runoff efficiency, dryness (ET/P), soil moisture, warming — standardized against its own WY1984–2024 baseline. 50 = baseline · +15 = one SD worse · most-recent 3-year mean.
Scope: Biophysical condition only, from observed/modelled hydrology. The full CRIA framework additionally integrates sociopolitical asset scores and stakeholder-agreed weights — from the project's separate co-development process, not estimated here.
