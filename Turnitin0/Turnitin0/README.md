## How to use Turnitin0

### A. Turnitin AI & plagiarism check

1. Open **[www.turnitin0.com](https://www.turnitin0.com)** and choose **Turnitin AI & Plagiarism Check**.
2. Upload a `.docx`, `.pdf`, or `.txt` file (English; typically **300–30,000** words; under **90 MB**).
3. Pay per scan (or use a prepaid pack / balance after signing in with Google).
4. Wait for processing — most orders finish in **5–10 minutes** (guaranteed within **30 minutes** even in rare queue spikes).
5. Download both **AI** and **similarity** PDFs from your task history, review flagged passages, then submit through your school portal when ready.

### B. AI Humanizer

1. On **[Turnitin0](https://www.turnitin0.com)**, open **Turnitin AI Humanizer**.
2. Upload a `.docx` or `.txt` draft (English; under **90 MB**).
3. Pay by word block (or use a prepaid word pack).
4. Download the humanized document, and optionally **re-scan** with the non-repository checker before official submission.
