Our Story

Bringing rigor to land-based carbon accounting

2025
Founded in Mauritius

GreenCarbon Analytics was founded in 2025 in the Republic of Mauritius. Our team combines expertise in life cycle assessment, agricultural science, and software engineering to solve a critical problem: the lack of credible, automated carbon data for land-based supply chains.

As CSRD, EUDR, and SBTi FLAG regulations tighten, companies face mounting pressure to measure and disclose the carbon impact of palm oil, soy, cocoa, cotton, timber, and other land-based ingredients. Yet purpose-built tools didn't exist.

We built that tool — and partnered with goodcarbon to offer a complete measure-reduce-offset workflow in a single platform.

"Our mission is to make land-based carbon data as accessible, credible, and actionable as possible — enabling companies to build genuinely sustainable supply chains."

— GreenCarbon Team

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a land-based carbon footprint?

A land-based carbon footprint measures the total greenhouse gas emissions associated with agricultural, forestry, or other land-derived products — from cultivation and harvesting through processing, transport, and packaging. Our calculator follows ISO 14040/44 methodology and reports across all 16 EF v3.1 impact categories.

How is this different from generic LCA tools?

Generic LCA tools require manual data entry and LCA expertise. GreenCarbon is purpose-built for land-based supply chains with pre-loaded emission factors for 500+ agricultural and forestry materials, AI-powered data extraction, and automated report generation aligned with ISO 14040/44.

Is your methodology validated?

Yes. Our Product Carbon Footprint methodology follows ISO 14040/44 and is currently undergoing SGS validation (expected Q4 2026). All emission factors are sourced from peer-reviewed databases including Ecoinvent 3.9.1 and Agribalyse 3.1.

How does the goodcarbon integration work?

After calculating your product's carbon footprint, you can match residual emissions to verified nature-based projects through our direct API integration with goodcarbon. Purchase offsets with one click and receive Swiss Re-insured retirement certificates with QR codes for verification.

Can I integrate this with my existing systems?

Yes. Our Business and Enterprise plans include API access for integration with ERP, PLM, and sustainability reporting systems. We also offer custom integrations for Enterprise clients including SSO/SAML and on-premise deployment options.

What regulations does this help me comply with?

GreenCarbon helps you comply with CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive), EUDR (EU Deforestation Regulation), EU Green Claims Directive, SBTi FLAG guidelines, CDP, and GRI standards. Our reports are export-ready for all major disclosure frameworks.

How accurate are the calculations?

We use Monte Carlo simulation for uncertainty quantification and provide data quality scores for each emission factor. Our methodology is aligned with ISO 14040/44 and uses the most current peer-reviewed databases. For carbon credit certification, we recommend field sampling validation.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes! Our Free plan includes 1 product calculation with full Product Carbon Footprint report and PDF export. Pro and Business plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Where is my data stored?

Data is stored on EU-based servers with GDPR compliance. Enterprise clients can opt for on-premise deployment. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Can you help with custom consulting?

Yes. Our Enterprise plan includes dedicated account management and custom consulting services. We also offer bespoke LCA studies, methodology development, and training programs for sustainability teams.
