🎬 MiniMax H3 Demo

Generate video with a synchronized stereo soundtrack from text, images, references and audio. This is a static demo showing the official example outputs of MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3.

Text-to-Video + Audio T2VA10s · 16:9

Text only → a full video and its soundtrack in one denoising pass. This example is a space-opera scene with an orchestral score, generated from the prompt in the official H3-Context-IR example.

First/Last-Frame-to-Video + Audio FL2VA10s · 16:9

Condition on a starting frame (and optionally an ending frame) to animate it into video with audio. Here a family ramen dinner comes alive with steam, motion and a koto-accompanied score.

Image-to-Video + Audio I2VA8s

First-frame-to-video: a single keyframe is animated forward, keeping the subject and scene consistent.

Reference-to-Video + Audio Ref2VA5s

Omni-reference: mixes up to 9 images, 3 videos and 3 audio clips. Here a subject speaks in sync with a reference voice, set to a reference soundtrack.

About this Space

This is a static, free Space. It showcases the official reproducible 768p outputs hosted in the MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3 repository. Running the real ~33B-parameter model requires a paid Gradio Space (PRO) plus ZeroGPU/GPU hardware with int8/NVFP4 quantization and CPU offload.
