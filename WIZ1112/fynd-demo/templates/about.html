About

FYND is a cross-modal fashion search demo that understands both words and images. Describe what you're looking for in plain language — a color, a mood, a fit — or attach a photo, and FYND finds the closest pieces in its catalog.

Dataset

The catalog covers about 12,000 clothing items and accessories from 29 independent Korean domestic brands, spanning tops, outerwear, pants, skirts, dresses, shoes, bags and accessories.

Features

  • Text search — describe items in natural language, e.g. "a black hooded puffer jacket with diamond quilting"
  • Image search — attach a photo to find visually similar pieces
  • Refine — nudge the results with follow-up prompts like "lighter blue" or "slim fitted"
  • Not for me (✕) — dismiss an item, and similar ones step back too
  • Wishlist — save pieces you love and revisit them anytime
