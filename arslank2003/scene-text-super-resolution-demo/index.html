How to interpret the demo

TextZoom provides real paired low-resolution and high-resolution scene-text images. CUTE80 is used as a synthetic cross-dataset evaluation containing curved and irregular text.

The dissertation compares a non-learning Bicubic baseline, general-purpose Real-ESRGAN, and the text-specific TSRN and C3-STISR methods.

The results demonstrate that the method producing the strongest visual or pixel-level reconstruction is not necessarily the method producing the most accurate reconstructed text.
