THE SCANDAL

The Draft

On August 19th, 2026, teams for the Nubwo Community Cup were revealed live on stream. Among the drafted players was Sinko, a young and promising Rainbow Six Siege talent. However, the community was shocked to discover who Sinko would be playing alongside.

The Controversy

Sinko was drafted onto a team with Lihjit, a player widely known in the community for serious transgressions — so severe that many refer to him by the moniker "PDF File". A player who, according to community consensus, should never have been allowed entry into the tournament.

"A player with this kind of history has no place in a community tournament. The fact that they were not only allowed in, but drafted onto a team with a young player like Sinko, is a failure of due diligence." — Community Statement

"This isn't about cancel culture. This is about basic safety and integrity in our community. When someone has crossed certain lines, there are no second chances in competitive spaces — especially not when young players are involved."

THE YOUNG PLAYER CAUGHT IN THE CROSSFIRE

SINKO

A young, up-and-coming player in the Rainbow Six Siege scene, Sinko found himself thrust into the spotlight for all the wrong reasons. Drafted onto a team without any say in his teammates, he now faces an impossible situation: compete alongside a player the community has deemed unsafe, or withdraw and lose his shot at tournament experience.

The community has rallied behind Sinko, emphasizing that the blame lies not with him, but with the tournament organizers who allowed this pairing to happen.
