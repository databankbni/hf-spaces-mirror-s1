Visual dashboard for the held-out TacEx Wipe causal replay and matched policy study.

The frozen replay result rejects the preregistered `Ours > RDP > DP` ordering:
Ours has the best expert-action imitation, while DP beats RDP and RDP has the
stronger tactile-correction mechanism read. The Space bundles the frozen
protocol, episode output, analysis, and full result under
`data/tacex_causal_replay_v1/`.

Data and provenance: https://huggingface.co/datasets/aaa1234zzz/roboprog-tacex-asymmetry
