Status 13 — LeVo 2 song generation, and the tokenization question

Two separate asks. One has a full result set; the other has not been started, and this page says so plainly rather than inventing a status.

1. LeVo 2 — where it stands

Done: a 10-generation evolutionary prompt search on four pilot genres, two controlled probe experiments, and a transfer test of the resulting recipe onto the 50 clustered genres. 2,523 clips judged in total.

The headline is a qualified one. The recipe works well on the genres it was evolved on and transfers only partially to the 50-genre set: solo-voice pass rate 46 % → 61 %, but all three criteria together only 31 %. Nine of fifty genres cannot be reached at all.

1.2 The two controlled probes — why negation cannot be used

Negation probe (4 genres × 6 clips per cell)

The manipulation checks are the point. Adding backing vocals should lower the solo score, and it does. But adding no backing vocals lowers it just as much. The model keys on the noun and drops the negation, so a negation is worse than saying nothing.

LeVo 2 wins on the judges (22 of 30 clips at 4 or better, against 4–5 for the ACE-Step variants and 0 for HeartMuLa) and loses on speed: 0.67× realtime, i.e. it takes about 44 s to make 30 s of audio, where ACE-Step turbo runs at 2.8× realtime. Note the aesthetic predictor disagrees with the judges — it ranks HeartMuLa top (4.12) and LeVo 2 near the bottom (2.41) while the judges rank them the other way round. For solo-voice work the aesthetic head should not be used as a selector.

What I need from you to start. “Tokenization” could mean at least three different jobs here, with very different costs:

  • Audio codec tokens (MOSS / DAC-VAE frames) over the audio, to train an LM on — GPU-bound, roughly the same order as a generation pass over the corpus.
  • Text tokenization of the captions and transcripts for an LM dataset — CPU-bound and cheap.
  • Packing into training shards (tokens + conditioning, WebDataset or parquet, bucketed by length) — the thing an actual training run consumes.

Tell me which one, on which of the four datasets, and I will scope it and run it. My default assumption if you just say “go” would be the third: codec tokens plus conditioning, packed into length-bucketed WebDataset shards, since that is what a training run needs and it subsumes the first.
